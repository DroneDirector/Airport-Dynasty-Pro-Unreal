# Phone and tablet in your hand - plan (owner, 2026-11-19)

Reference: `Art/Reference/From-Phone/2026-11-19/phone-hold-reference.jpg` (his own hand holding a phone).

## The rules (his words, short)
- Picking up -> holding runs fluidly and responsively.
- Held exactly as in the photo: right hand, phone upright in the palm, thumb on the right side edge, fingers round the back.
- Scrolling and moving between items/apps/home screen with the SIDE BUTTON: the thumb gently moves on it, a highlight
  box steps item to item.
- The device stays fixed in your view; the mouse moves the "+" pointer ON its screen (default on every pick-up, so you
  always see where you click). Middle mouse = look around freely while still holding it; middle again = the "+" back.
  Same for the tablet.
- The "+" speed follows how fast you move the mouse (done, 98695b4).
- Esc / Circle always puts the device all the way down (done, 98695b4).
- The background blurred like a portrait photo while you hold the phone (as the tablet).
