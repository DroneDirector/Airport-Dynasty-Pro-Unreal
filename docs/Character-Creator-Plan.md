# The character creator plan

The new career's creator section: your looks, your tattoos, your pet, your partner. 2026-11-15.

Everything here follows the steps you set: New game, then "Who are you?" (she on the left, he on the right), then
the creator, then pets, then married or not and your partner's looks, then the airline founding, the difficulty and
Start game. Today the looks, pet and partner steps show placeholder panels; this plan fills them.

## A. The look of the screens

- **The same stage as "Who are you?":** dark room, floor fog to the knees, the neon tubes gone, the pink glow lighting you. You stay where you stood; the camera does not move away.
- **The menu on the free side:** she chosen, the menu on the right; he chosen, on the left. It is the title screen's style: categories as neon title bars, a glass window beside them with the choices as tiles, Audiowide letters, cyan and pink.
- **You are always free in view,** never behind the menu. The camera frames what you are changing: the whole body, the face (hair, brows, beard, eyes), or the feet (shoes). The fog clears round you the first time you come to shoes and stays cleared.
- **Portrait blur:** you sharp, the room soft.
- **Music:** the title music plays on through every step and fades at Start game; the mute button is on every step.
- **Controls:** mouse, keyboard and pad. Pad buttons are shown only when a pad is connected. Back and Next on every step; Back plays the animations in reverse.

## B. Your looks: the categories

| Category | What you choose | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| Face | a face from the library | built ahead (see E) |
| Skin | skin tone in steps | built ahead |
| Freckles | none, a few, many - its own choice | built ahead (part of the skin) |
| Age look | younger to older, if Unreal 5.7's MetaHuman skin offers it | to check first |
| Body | body types (slim to heavy) | built ahead |
| Height | a range round the body's own height | live: the skeleton scaled, limits to test |
| Hair | the hairstyle (true MetaHuman strand hair) | to check: swapping live or built ahead |
| Hair colour | colour, and how grey | live: the hair's own colour settings |
| Brows | the brow style and colour | same as hair |
| Beard and moustache | style (he; she may choose none) | same as hair |
| Eyes | eye colour | to check: live or built ahead |
| Clothes | your first outfit from the wardrobe | live: the wardrobe's outfits |
| Tattoos | see C | live, ready now |

"Built ahead" means each face, skin and body combination is a person built in Unreal before the game ships: that is
how MetaHumans work here. Measured: about 4 minutes of PC time per person. "Live" means it changes instantly while you
choose, nothing built.

## C. Tattoos (in the creator, and later at the tattooist)

- **Optional:** you can skip it, and add or remove any tattoo later at the tattooist (also the ones chosen here).
- **Ten places:** left and right arm (down over the back of the hand), left and right leg, the fingers of each hand (never the nails), the neck's left side, right side, back and throat. Several at once; a place can hold more than one design.
- **Designs:** 14 today; the goal is 50 per kind of place (arms, legs, neck, fingers), colour and black-and-grey. Measured: about 1.75 minutes per design including the in-game check, before your corrections.
- **How you choose:** pick a place, then a design from the tiles (a colour / black-and-grey filter on top). The camera goes to that place, and the design appears on your skin as you hover over it. At most six on the body and six on the neck at once.
- **Status:** the skin layers, the places, saving, adding and removing all work in the game (0.17.15). What's left is this screen.

## D. Your pet (the step after your looks)

- **Choose:** a dog, a cat, or none. Then the breed (about 20, popular ones, different sizes, and mixes), the coat colour, and a name.
- **Shown live:** when the step opens, the first pet in the list comes walking in and sits down beside you (breathing, tail). Move the selection on and it plays in reverse: it stands up and walks back out, and the next one walks in and sits. The one you choose stays sitting beside you.
- **Dogs and cats, each their own section,** filled automatically: every dog or cat model added later (put in its folder and imported) shows up in its section by itself - no list to edit.
- **Status:** your German Shepherd and house cat (your Meshy models) are in, reduced to 200,000 and 120,000 faces with their textures; their skeletons and moves are being made (stand with breathing and tail, sit, lie flat, walk fast; the dog's fast run on command; the cat's rare roll-over). Until they are in the game this step shows its placeholder panel.
- **Swappable later:** your save keeps only your choice (kind, breed, coat, name); a better model under the same name shows up in games already started.

## E. Your partner (married or not)

- **Married or single.** If married: your partner's looks made with the same creator (the same categories, tattoos included), and a name.
- Your partner is built from the same library as you, so nothing extra has to be built for them.

## F. What is saved

Your choices are saved as names (face, skin, body, hairstyle, colours, height, outfit, tattoos, pet, partner), never
the models. So an improved model or a new choice can be added later without rebuilding anything else: one line in
the list, and old saves get the better model at their next load.

## G. Later in the game

- **The barber:** changes your hair, colour and grey, brows, beard and moustache. Kept in your save.
- **The tattooist:** adds and removes tattoos. Kept in your save.
- **The wardrobe:** your clothes, as today.
