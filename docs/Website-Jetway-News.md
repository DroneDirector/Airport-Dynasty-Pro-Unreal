## Jetways built like the real thing

The jetways at Airport Dynasty Pro are built to follow real airport passenger bridges, in their proportions,
dimensions, structure and the way their lights behave.

- **Telescoping tunnels in the real order.** Three tunnel sections slide into one another like a telescope: the
  smallest at the terminal end, the biggest at the cab, where the wheels and lift columns are. Each section is only a
  little larger than the one inside it, as on real bridges, so they glide in and out without ever cutting into each other.
- **Real structure.** The steel posts and X-bracing sit in the walls, the roofs are slim, and at every joint the larger
  section's frame wraps neatly around the smaller one, with a sliding floor plate so the walk stays flat.
- **Seamless from the terminal to the aircraft.** The corridor from the terminal, the round rotunda and the first
  tunnel share the same inside width, and the tunnel opens up slightly towards the aircraft.
- **Detail you can walk up to.** The pleated rubber seal at the cab, the joint seals, the steel frames and cable trays
  under the floor, the air-conditioning unit hung under the tunnel with its two yellow hoses, and the stepped sills under
  the windows are all modelled.
- **Real movement.** The jetway drives at real speeds, extends, lifts and turns its cab to the aircraft's front door,
  with warning beacons, sounds and the hoses moving along with it.
