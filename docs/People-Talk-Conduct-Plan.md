# Airport Dynasty Pro - talking to people, hiring and unruly passengers

Owner's design, 16 November 2026. To-do items 69a-69i. Comes after the people animations (14), living passengers (15), conversations and voices (18) and the security uniforms (13b).

## The design, in order

### 1. Outline on people
- Looking at a MetaHuman shows the same thin glow as other things you can use.

### 2. E at a person opens two choices
- Just talk: pick from ready-made questions. They answer in their own way, matching the question.
  - On personal topics some refuse ("I'd rather not talk about it"), others answer.
- Ask them to work for you: 13% say yes, with their own reasons.
  - The rest say "thanks, but I'm not looking for work".

### 3. A misbehaving passenger
- E shows only choices about their behaviour.
- You pick one of two ways to deal with it.

### 4. If they get angry or violent
- You can try to calm them down.
  - 75% calm down, apologise humbly, promise to behave, and then do.
  - 25% won't listen: you can call airport security.

### 5. Security arrives
- They appear somewhere out of your sight, take a few seconds, then walk to the passenger.
- You hear them once they are within 10 m, sometimes only closer.
- Security and the passenger talk back and forth, usually 20-30 seconds, sometimes less.

### 6. If security can't calm it down
- They call the police.
- The police and the passenger talk back and forth for a while.

### 7. If it still gets out of hand
- Usually the passenger gives in, is handcuffed and walked out of the airport.
- 15% of the time it turns violent:
  - The police draw the stun gun and give a last warning to get on the ground.
  - If the passenger obeys: cuffs, walked out.
  - If they refuse, the police say "I will use force".
  - The passenger always answers with a random defiant line, such as "And so what!", "Go ahead then!" or "You wouldn't dare!".
  - The police then use the stun gun.

### 8. The stun gun, as in real life
- The stun gun makes the muscles lock for a 5-second cycle (Axon, maker of the TASER).
- The moment it hits: the body goes rigid, arched, arms pulled tight. They fall stiffly without catching themselves, and cry out.
- When the 5 seconds end: the muscles let go. Limp on the floor, breathing hard, dazed.
- Then the police walk over, cuff them face down on the floor, lift them up and walk them out.
- Rigid first, limp after - not a floppy ragdoll from the start.

### 9. Man or woman
- The passenger can be a man or a woman, chosen at random.

### 10. Voices
- With you, a violent passenger is already a bit aggressive in voice and tone.
- Once security or the police are involved, the real aggression kicks in, far stronger.
- More cooperative passengers sound less so, varied at random but never too far.

### 11. Body language
- Violent passengers look way more violent once security or the police are involved: agitated, pacing, pointing, throwing their arms about.
- They never hit a security guard, a police officer or you.
