## Why: capacity (TC-1)

- **The busiest hour** (from the stands): about 1 685 departures and 1 685 arrivals. Of these, 455 an hour are non-Schengen (gates 13-16). An A380 and a B777 landing together bring about 750 people at once.
- **Short today:** check-in handles 480 bags an hour, but about 1 100 are needed. Security handles 900 passengers an hour, but about 1 700 are needed.
- **Missing today:** all of passport control (in and out), a non-Schengen claim hall and an arrivals hall for the east gates.
- **Already enough:** the gate lounges and the west claim and arrivals halls meet the IATA optimum.

## Real rules you did not ask for (I am telling you, as agreed)

- **Schengen Borders Code:** passengers to and from non-Schengen countries must pass border checks. This is why gates 13-16 sit behind passport control.
- **EU 2015/1998 (aviation security):** arriving passengers from non-EU airports must not mix with screened departing passengers. This is why there is an arrivals corridor and sliding glass screens at gates 14-16.
- **EU 2015/1998:** staff must also be screened before entering airside. This is why there is a staff security checkpoint.
- **EU 1107/2006:** airports must have marked assistance points for passengers with reduced mobility. One is in the East Hall, plus the wide e-gate lanes.
