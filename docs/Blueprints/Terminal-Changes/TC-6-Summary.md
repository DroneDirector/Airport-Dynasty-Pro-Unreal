# Terminal changes for real departures and arrivals - summary (to-do 213)

## Your rules, kept

- **Grand Hall** stays 72 x 30 m = 2160 m²; nothing new in its queue zone. **Every room** stays where it is; **no gate, stand or jetway** moves.
- **East wing** used in full (20 540 m²). **Belts re-routed**: the Grand Hall tunnel runs on under the bag-drop row; a new East Hall tunnel west under x 156 rises in the make-up hall; the east claim belts are fed from the baggage hall.
- **Facade**: the wave roof runs on east to x 551 (same section, crests every 30 m), with the Grand Hall's floor-to-roof glass and tree pillars all along.

## Why: capacity (TC-1)

- **The busiest hour** (from the stands): about 1 685 departures and 1 685 arrivals. Of these, 455 an hour are non-Schengen (gates 13-16). An A380 and a B777 landing together bring about 750 people at once.
- **Short today:** check-in handles 480 bags an hour, but about 1 100 are needed. Security handles 900 passengers an hour, but about 1 700 are needed.
- **Missing today:** all of passport control (in and out), a non-Schengen claim hall and an arrivals hall for the east gates.
- **Already enough:** the gate lounges and the west claim and arrivals halls meet the IATA optimum.

## What changes

1. **Grand Hall (same size):** 12 kiosks between the entrances, 6 bag-drop machines at the east end of the islands, a service desk on the east wall, boarding-pass e-gates at both security doors, trolley bays.
2. **Security:** the same 6 lanes become automated lanes (ATRS, 280 an hour each, 1 680 in all) in the same room.
3. **Link gallery** (x 107-156, 4 m deep, glass): a landside walk between the Grand Hall and the East Hall. It is flush with the vestibules.
4. **East Hall** (x 156-228, 72 x 40 m, glass to the wave roof): long-haul check-in (8 desks, 4 bag-drop, oversize drop), meeters and greeters with a meeting rail, a café, shops, 5 car-rental counters, info and PRM point, first aid, toilets, family and quiet rooms.
5. **East wing, behind a landside promenade** (242 x 6 m, glass front, shops) that leads to the coach station:
   - Customs green/red (x 228-236).
   - Non-Schengen claim hall (x 236-312) with 2 wide-body belts, lost and found, an oversize belt and trolley bays.
   - Passport control in (6 booths, 8 e-gates) with its queue hall, under the arrivals mezzanine.
   - Staff and operations (x 372-470): crew centre, canteen, lockers, staff security (2 lanes), ground handling and airline offices, police and border guard.
   - Coach and bus station (x 470-551, 4 220 m²).
6. **West claim wing:** lost-and-found desk, oversize belt, trolley bays. It already has customs and an arrivals hall.
7. **Floor 2:** passport control out (3 booths, 4 e-gates) in a glass wall across the walkway at x 240-248 before gate 13; a glazed 4 m arrivals corridor for gates 13-16; a 42 x 12 m arrivals mezzanine with escalators, stair and lift down to passport control in.

## Real rules you did not ask for (telling you, as agreed)

- **Schengen Borders Code:** non-Schengen passengers pass border checks, so gates 13-16 sit behind passport control.
- **EU 2015/1998:** arrivals from non-EU airports must not mix with screened departures (arrivals corridor, sliding screens); staff are screened before airside (staff security).
- **EU 1107/2006:** marked assistance points for reduced-mobility passengers (East Hall; wide e-gate lanes).
