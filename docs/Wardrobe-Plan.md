# Clothes for the people: the plan and what it costs (2026-11-12)

The MetaHuman kit on the PC has one outfit (a grey tee and shorts), so every garment is made by us in Blender,
fitted to the MetaHuman body, textured, and saved as a wardrobe item. Putting a garment on a person is the
3-minute local re-fit (no cloud, nothing of the person's face or skin is redone). Prints and colours on a garment
are pictures only: 10-20 minutes each, no new garment. Everything is done in 2-3 hour sessions.

## C. Prints and colours (variety for almost nothing)

A sports car on a tee, a Hawaiian print, stripes, a jeans wash, a jacket colour: 10-20 minutes each, drawn by us
(no real brands or logos). 50 variants at launch: about 12 h. More can be added at any time.

## D. Your wardrobe (changing clothes in the game)

Yes, possible: your own character is built in each of your outfits once (3 minutes each), and a wardrobe in your
office (or your home later) swaps between them on the spot, E / X to use. About 4 h for the wardrobe itself.
New outfits later: one 3-minute build each.

## E. Clothes that glitch (the body poking through)

How it is prevented: at the re-fit the garment is resized to that person's body, and the skin under the garment is
hidden, so a covered body part cannot poke through. Problems show at the edges (cuffs, collars, hems) and in
extreme poses (sitting, crouching, a sprint). Fixing a garment: 30-60 minutes on the garment itself, then the
3-minute re-fit for the people wearing it. A test scene that runs every garment through walk, sit and run and takes
pictures: about 1 h per batch of garments, done as each batch is made. Budget at launch: about 8 h of fit testing.

## F. Adding a complete new set after launch

One garment: 1.5-2 h plus its prints. A full set (jacket, shirt, trousers, shoes): about 6 h. Dressing the people
who get it: 3 minutes each, unattended. It ships as a small content-pack update, not a full new game download.
