# THE AIRPLANE TEST (owner 2026-11-28): the A320 at the gate, the take-off roll, 10,000 ft (the -AirplaneTest jump 20 s after
# lift-off, 8 s straight, a 180 deg right turn at about 24 deg of bank), the landing. At sunset, a few clouds.
#   bash Tools/airplane_test.sh video    the whole run recorded (60 fps, the game's own sound), no pictures (-ViewsNoShots)
#   bash Tools/airplane_test.sh photos   the same run, a 4K / screen-size PNG at every shot4k (Saved/Screenshots/run)
# Every camera follows the aircraft (orbit / look: PerfViews.cpp header); the holds are the same in both runs.
# Sunset: day 172 (21 June) at Chablais (46.36 N 6.91 E, UTC+2): the sun 4.6 deg up at 20:48 (SunClock::SunAt), 1.1 deg at 21:15.
cmd r.VSync 0
framegen on
speed 1 1
day 172
hour 20.8
weather 1
wait 25
lives
park
jetway 4 dock
wait 45
jetway 4 cam wide
# ===== 1 gate: the A320 at gate 4, its right side (the jetway is on its left), orbit then low and close
orbit 38 60 60 g2.5 0 A320
wait 2
sound record airplane-test
orbit 38 60 165 g2.5 22 A320
wait 4
shot4k gate-orbit-front
wait 9
shot4k gate-orbit-rear
orbit 24 120 95 g1.6 10 A320
wait 6
shot4k gate-low-mains
wait 3
# ===== 2 take-off: lined up, the roll low beside the wheels, rotation, lift-off, the gear going up
flight takeoff a320
orbit 30 60 60 g1.6 0 HB-TOFF
wait 2
shot4k toff-lineup
flightwait 60 HB-TOFF roll
orbit 28 60 105 g1.6 12 HB-TOFF
wait 9
shot4k toff-roll-wheels
wait 3
orbit 46 95 95 g1.6 0 HB-TOFF
flightwait 90 HB-TOFF climb
shot4k toff-liftoff
orbit 45 100 150 3 10 HB-TOFF
wait 5
shot4k toff-gear-up
# ===== 3 10,000 ft: tracking, round to ahead, the banked turn (wings dip), behind, level again
flightwait 40 HB-TOFF high
orbit 42 95 95 2 0 HB-TOFF
wait 1
shot4k high-track
orbit 48 95 -15 3 6 HB-TOFF
flightwait 30 HB-TOFF turn
wait 4
shot4k turn-ahead-bank
orbit 46 -15 75 5 14 HB-TOFF
wait 7
shot4k turn-inside-wing-down
orbit 55 150 215 4 16 HB-TOFF
wait 8
shot4k turn-behind
orbit 48 -40 -20 1 8 HB-TOFF
flightwait 70 HB-TOFF high
wait 2
shot4k turn-level-again
# ===== 4 landing: approach from ahead and beside, low by the touchdown zone (main gear smoke, the nose let down gently), rollout
flight land a320 60
orbit 50 -35 -35 4 0 HB-LAND
wait 4
shot4k land-approach-ahead
orbit 42 -35 80 2 14 HB-LAND
wait 10
shot4k land-approach-side
wait 18
orbit 36 105 105 g1.6 0 HB-LAND
flightwait 45 HB-LAND rollout
shot4k land-touchdown-smoke
orbit 34 100 115 g1.6 6 HB-LAND
wait 3
shot4k land-nose-down
orbit 40 115 165 g1.8 10 HB-LAND
wait 5
shot4k land-rollout-reversers
wait 4
# ===== end
sound stop
wait 2
lives
parkoff
hour live
weather live
quit
