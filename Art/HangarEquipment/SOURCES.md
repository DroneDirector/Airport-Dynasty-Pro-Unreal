# Hangar equipment - sources

Our own models (to-do 364 A), built by the scripts in this folder. No brands, no bought or downloaded models.

Photo-scanned surface textures (CC0, ambientCG.com, the same sets as the pushback tug, read from Art/GPU/Textures):
PaintedMetal016, Paint004, Metal011, Metal027, DiamondPlate008A, Rubber004 (tyres, castor wheels, hoses),
Plastic006, Plastic013A.

Own pictures: Textures/HQ_Paint*_Color.jpg, HQ_WarningStripes_Color.jpg (hq_lib.py), HQ_GaugeFace.png,
HQ_Label_*.png (make_detail_textures.py).

Reference photos looked at (not copied), Wikimedia Commons, US government work, public domain:
- "US Navy 070430-N-4420S-029 ... operates a nitrogen cart to fill the tires of an F-A-18C Hornet" (nitrogen cart:
  bottles lying in the frame, hinged lid, panel with regulator, gauges and steel lines)
- "U.S. Air Force Hydraulic Test Stand inspection.jpg" (hydraulic cart panel: round gauges, T-handle valves, quick
  couplings, black hoses with brass/steel fittings)
- "332nd Expeditionary Maintenance Squadron AGE Operations (9172402).jpg" (ground equipment panel: white-faced gauges,
  T-valves, sight glasses, coiled black hoses)
- Batch 1: "374 AW MXS, AMXS perform engine maintenance operations in Sri Lanka" (engine stand).
No usable free photo was found for the wheel dolly, the tyre inflation cage, the oil cart and the hangar GPU: they are
built from published sizes (A320 tyres 46x17.0R20 main, 30x8.8R15 nose; 50 l 200 bar nitrogen bottles; 3000 psi
hydraulics; 400 Hz 115/200 V 90 kVA ground power with its 6-pin aircraft plug).
