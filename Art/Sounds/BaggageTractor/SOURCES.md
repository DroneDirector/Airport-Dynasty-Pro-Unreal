# Electric baggage tow tractor sounds - sources (Creative Commons 0, checked on each sound's own page 2026-11-21; Freesound HQ previews in raw/, cut by Tools/make_baggage_tractor_sounds.py to mono 48 kHz)
- BTMotorLoop.wav - two layers: "ElectricMotor.wav" by derjuli, https://freesound.org/people/derjuli/sounds/824119/ (CC0) - an electric motor's steady hum, its steadiest 8.5 s; plus "Electric Scooter Loop.WAV" by pkri, https://freesound.org/people/pkri/sounds/587511/ (CC0) - the scooter motor's whine (1-5.3 s) played an octave down at half level for a bigger traction motor. Made a seamless loop; the game pitches it with speed.
- BTStart.wav - two layers: "Contactor/motor overload relay noises" by tt_runscript, https://freesound.org/people/tt_runscript/sounds/807384/ (CC0) - one of the loud contactor thunks (21.86 s) as the main contactor pulling in; plus "Electric car start-up and drive away" by evsecrets, https://freesound.org/people/evsecrets/sounds/324119/ (CC0) - the Peugeot iOn's ready-to-drive beep (13.8-14.7 s), 0.45 s after the thunk.
- BTStop.wav - the contactor thunk at 34.34 s of the same tt_runscript recording (the contactor dropping out), with the BTMotorLoop sound slowed to a quarter speed and faded under it (the whine winding down).
- BTReverseBeep.wav - "backup_beeper.wav" by Mihacappy, https://freesound.org/people/Mihacappy/sounds/878172/ (CC0) - a generic machinery backup beeper (made by the author in Audacity, not a recording), one beep + gap, loopable as it is. (Different from the pushback tug's Disasteradio beeper.)
- BTDoorOpen.wav / BTDoorClose.wav - "Small Car Door Open and Close" by joeffl, https://freesound.org/people/joeffl/sounds/853133/ (CC0) - one recording from inside a small car, split into the door opening and the door shutting.
- BTHorn.wav - "Beeping car horn from inside car, engine off" by misperpodcast, https://freesound.org/people/misperpodcast/sounds/733553/ (CC0) - a 2008 Fiat Panda's electric horn, its second (longer) beep.
- BTHitchPin.wav - two layers: "METAL SFX & FOLEY, Metal slide, clunk and clink (door bolt).wav" by Alexbuk, https://freesound.org/people/Alexbuk/sounds/391794/ (CC0) - a steel bolt sliding home with its clunk (12.45-13.1 s); plus "Metal pole, dropping, set down, metal ring.wav" by SpliceSound, https://freesound.org/people/SpliceSound/sounds/188206/ (CC0) - a heavy metal pole's set-down impact (13.04 s) at half level under the clunk for weight.
- BTPinOut.wav - the same Alexbuk door-bolt recording, a bolt sliding out ending in a small clink (20.3-21.0 s).
- (also downloaded, kept for later, all CC0: "buggy motorized cart pass by close interior hallway sooo noisy.flac" by kyles, https://freesound.org/people/kyles/sounds/635483/ (golfcart.mp3 - a pass-by, broadband, not steady enough for a loop); "forklift.wav" by soundman9826, https://freesound.org/people/soundman9826/sounds/571002/ and "Forklift starting" https://freesound.org/people/soundman9826/sounds/570979/ (forklift*.mp3 - a 47 Hz firing tone, sounds like a combustion/LPG forklift, not electric); "Machine_Electric_Stacker_Steering_Control_Sequence_Stereo" by Nox_Sound, https://freesound.org/people/Nox_Sound/sounds/877386/ (stacker.mp3 - real electric stacker motor, but only 1.5 s bursts); "kontaktorok.wav" by Szntndr, https://freesound.org/people/Szntndr/sounds/644035/ (kontaktor.mp3 - AC contactors with mains hum between clicks); "Car Door open and close 2.wav" by NachtmahrTV, https://freesound.org/people/NachtmahrTV/sounds/556691/ (door2.mp3 - clipped); "fietsbel ebike HORN speedbike" by gecop, https://freesound.org/people/gecop/sounds/543034/ (ebikehorn.mp3 - 2.1 kHz, too toy-like); "Drop_Metallic_Rod.wav" by mickyman5000, https://freesound.org/people/mickyman5000/sounds/340615/ (rod.mp3 - light, bright); "Hyundai Kona electric car - interior - car turning off - silence" by leonelmail, https://freesound.org/people/leonelmail/sounds/579562/ (konaoff.mp3 - very quiet, no clear off sound); "Electric Go-Karts-indoor" by JohnsonBrandEditing, https://freesound.org/people/JohnsonBrandEditing/sounds/173931/ (gokart.mp3 - a hall full of passing karts, not steady))
- (looked at, not used: "Electric Scooter" by IENBA, https://freesound.org/people/IENBA/sounds/697379/ - CC0, a free wheel spin only, not downloaded; "electric car - bmw i3 - internal sounds + driving" by tferrino, https://freesound.org/people/tferrino/sounds/405284/ - marked CC0 but its description adds "don't re-distribute" conditions, so left out; "electric bus" by Rahmmovie, https://freesound.org/people/Rahmmovie/sounds/857713/ - CC0, with voices and brakes, not downloaded)
