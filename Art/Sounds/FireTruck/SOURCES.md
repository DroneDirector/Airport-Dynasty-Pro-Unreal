# Fire truck sounds - sources (Creative Commons 0, checked on each page 2026-11-19; Freesound HQ previews, made mono 48 kHz)
- FtSiren.wav - "siren_of_the_fire_truck.wav" by monosfera, https://freesound.org/people/monosfera/sounds/569843/ (CC0) -
  a real fire truck's two-tone siren recorded close; 4 whole cycles (16.32-20.32 s), the end crossfaded into the
  recording just before the start, so it loops without a seam. Replaces the made-up electronic wail.
- FtHorn.wav - "air horn close and loud.WAV" by goose278, https://freesound.org/people/goose278/sounds/174819/ (CC0) -
  a truck's air horn up close, one blast (2.56-3.10 s) with its own fade.
