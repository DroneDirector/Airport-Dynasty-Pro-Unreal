# Baggage cart sounds - sources (Creative Commons 0, checked on each sound's own page 2026-11-21; Freesound HQ previews in raw/, cut by Tools/make_baggage_cart_sounds.py to mono 48 kHz)
- BCRollLoop.wav - two layers: "Rolling Metal Cart/Table" by AcousticEel, https://freesound.org/people/AcousticEel/sounds/877756/ (CC0) - a steel cart rolling, its steadiest 6.4 s (11.25 s on); plus "R29-16-Large Cart or Ore Car Roll By.wav" by craigsmith, https://freesound.org/people/craigsmith/sounds/481117/ (CC0) - the heavy rumble at the closest point of the roll-by, slowed 15 % and mixed under at 45 %. Seamless 6 s loop; the game pitches and levels it with speed (silent at rest).
- BCDrawbarClank.wav - "Crowbar Drop 2 Rear.wav" by Starvolt, https://freesound.org/people/Starvolt/sounds/189221/ (CC0) - a heavy steel bar dropping onto concrete: the drawbar let down / springing up against its stop.
- BCPinIn.wav - "Metal Latch Latching" by deleted_user_7146007, https://freesound.org/people/deleted_user_7146007/sounds/383797/ (CC0) - its loudest latch: the coupling pin dropping home through the eye.
- BCPinOut.wav - "Metal Gate Latch 02" by NapierFreesound, https://freesound.org/people/NapierFreesound/sounds/710871/ (CC0) - the first latch lift: the pin pulled up.
- BCCurtainUp.wav / BCCurtainDown.wav - "Tarpaulin - Mouvements" by Vrymaa, https://freesound.org/people/Vrymaa/sounds/785112/ (CC0) - the first tarpaulin movement (rolling the curtain up) and the longest later one (pulling it down).
- BCCurtainStrap.wav - "Quickly pulling a plastic tarp off something.wav" by DrDufus, https://freesound.org/people/DrDufus/sounds/495400/ (CC0) - the sheet snapped tight as the straps are hooked.
