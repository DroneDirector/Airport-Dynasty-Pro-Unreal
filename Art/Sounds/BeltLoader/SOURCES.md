# Electric belt loader sounds - sources (Creative Commons 0, checked on each sound's own page; Freesound HQ previews in raw/, cut by Tools/make_belt_loader_sounds.py to mono 48 kHz)
- BLDriveLoop.wav - the electric baggage tractor's motor recipe played 10 % slower (a heavier machine): "ElectricMotor.wav" by derjuli, https://freesound.org/people/derjuli/sounds/824119/ (CC0) - the steadiest 8.5 s of the hum; plus "Electric Scooter Loop.WAV" by pkri, https://freesound.org/people/pkri/sounds/587511/ (CC0) - the whine an octave down at 45 %. Seamless loop; the game pitches it with speed.
- BLBeltLoop.wav - "Industrial Conveyor Belt" by Kinoton, https://freesound.org/people/Kinoton/sounds/519027/ (CC0) - a package-centre conveyor, its steadiest 20 s; plus "G40-04-Electric Motor with Resonant Clattering.wav" by craigsmith, https://freesound.org/people/craigsmith/sounds/438652/ (CC0) - the rollers' clatter at 35 % under it. Seamless loop.
- BLBeltStart.wav - the same craigsmith recording's first 3 s (the belt motor winding up with its clank), running into the conveyor loop.
- BLBoomUpLoop.wav / BLBoomDownLoop.wav - "hydraulics.wav" by nuckan, https://freesound.org/people/nuckan/sounds/212941/ (CC0, checked 2026-11-21) - a forklift's lift hydraulics (pump + oil flow), the steadiest 2.5 s made a loop; down = the same 12 % slower and softer (the boom lowers through the valve under its own weight).
- BLBoomStart.wav / BLBoomStop.wav - the pump contactor pulling in / dropping out ("Contactor/motor overload relay noises" by tt_runscript, https://freesound.org/people/tt_runscript/sounds/807384/ (CC0), 21.84 s and 34.32 s) with the hydraulic loop fading in / pitching down as the valve closes.
- BLReverseBeep.wav - "Truck Reverse Beeper.wav" by Disasteradio, https://freesound.org/people/Disasteradio/sounds/197166/ (CC0) - loopable.
- BLHorn.wav - "Beeping car horn from inside car, engine off" by misperpodcast, https://freesound.org/people/misperpodcast/sounds/733553/ (CC0) - a small electric horn, the second (longer) beep.
- BLDoorOpen.wav / BLDoorClose.wav - "Small Car Door Open and Close" by joeffl, https://freesound.org/people/joeffl/sounds/853133/ (CC0) - split into the opening and the shutting.
- (raw/stacker.mp3 - "Machine_Electric_Stacker_Steering_Control_Sequence_Stereo" by Nox_Sound, https://freesound.org/people/Nox_Sound/sounds/877386/ (CC0) - copied as a pump-motor candidate, not used: too quiet / bursts only.)
In the game every sound is placed at its source (drive at the chassis, belt along the boom, hydraulics at the rams, beeper at the rear) and heard as from where you stand (the jetway sounds' distance + occlusion treatment).
