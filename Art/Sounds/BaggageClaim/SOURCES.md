# Baggage claim sounds: sources

All sounds are Creative Commons 0. The licence was checked on each page on 2026-11-16. They were made from Freesound HQ previews, mono 48 kHz, by `make_claim_sounds.py`.

- **BeltStart.wav:** "G40-04-Electric Motor with Resonant Clattering.wav" by craigsmith, https://freesound.org/people/craigsmith/sounds/438652/ (CC0). The first 6 s: the motor winding up, with the clank.
- **BeltRun.wav:** "Industrial Conveyor Belt" by Kinoton, https://freesound.org/people/Kinoton/sounds/519027/ (CC0). Recorded close up in a package centre. Cut to 20 s with seamlessly crossfaded ends, so it loops.
- **BeltClack.wav:** "Belt Click Clap" by casperhazebeats, https://freesound.org/people/casperhazebeats/sounds/827583/ (CC0). One plate joint clack.
- **Alternative running loop (not used):** "Conveyor belt" by TiesWijnen, https://freesound.org/people/TiesWijnen/sounds/478247/ (CC0). A synthesized supermarket belt, loopable. Kept in `raw/`.

In the game, every belt sound is placed at its belt and heard as from where you stand. It fades with distance, and is muffled behind walls and from outside the hall (the jetway sounds' treatment).
