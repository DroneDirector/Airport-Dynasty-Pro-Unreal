# Ground power unit sounds - sources (Creative Commons 0, checked on each sound's own page 2026-11-21; Freesound HQ previews in raw/, cut by Tools/make_gpu_sounds.py to mono 48 kHz)
- GPUStart.wav - "Diesel engine starting revving and stopping" by chlund, https://freesound.org/people/chlund/sounds/500193/ (CC0) - the cranking, the catch and the settle to idle (first 9.5 s; the revving after it left out). The game cross-fades it into GPUIdle.
- GPUIdle.wav - "large diesel emergency generator is running" by Garuda1982, https://freesound.org/people/Garuda1982/sounds/606933/ (CC0) - a large diesel generator set running; its steadiest 10 s, made a seamless loop.
- GPUStop.wav - "Running mobile generator then throttle down" by PostProdDog, https://freesound.org/people/PostProdDog/sounds/536509/ (CC0) - the last part: 3 s of running, then the run-down and stop.
- GPUDoorOpen.wav / GPUDoorClose.wav - "Cabinet_Door_Metal_Plastic_Open_Close.wav" by LamaMakesMusic, https://freesound.org/people/LamaMakesMusic/sounds/403539/ (CC0) - one recording split into the latch/hinge opening and the panel shutting (the canopy's hinged access doors and the control-panel door).
- GPUPlugConnect.wav - "Metal radio plug" by R2dxu, https://freesound.org/people/R2dxu/sounds/797795/ (CC0) - its heaviest plug-in event: the connector pushed home in the aircraft's receptacle.
- GPUPlugDisconnect.wav - "Unplug" by Maajora, https://freesound.org/people/Maajora/sounds/432906/ (CC0) - the catch letting go and the plug sliding out.
- (also downloaded, kept for later: "Generator StartUp, Run, Shutdown.wav" by JeffWojo, https://freesound.org/people/JeffWojo/sounds/169940/; "Small Diesel Generator" by jameswrowles, https://freesound.org/people/jameswrowles/sounds/516759/; "Cabinet door" by zehmx, https://freesound.org/people/zehmx/sounds/322862/; "Plug Inserting into Electrical Socket" by _MC5_, https://freesound.org/people/_MC5_/sounds/594301/ - all CC0)
