# Pushback tug sounds - sources (Creative Commons 0, checked on each sound's own page 2026-11-21; Freesound HQ previews in raw/, cut by Tools/make_tug_sounds.py to mono 48 kHz)
- TugStart.wav - "tractor-engine-start.wav" by radwoc, https://freesound.org/people/radwoc/sounds/256808/ (CC0) - a diesel tractor's engine starting.
- TugIdle.wav - "Back Hoe Tractor.wav" by DrDufus, https://freesound.org/people/DrDufus/sounds/462878/ (CC0) - heavy diesel machinery idling; its steadiest 8 s, made a seamless loop.
- TugStop.wav - the same idle, slowed and faded as a diesel runs down when the fuel is cut.
- TugReverseBeep.wav - "Truck Reverse Beeper.wav" by Disasteradio, https://freesound.org/people/Disasteradio/sounds/197166/ (CC0) - a truck's reversing beeper, loopable.
- TugDoorOpen.wav / TugDoorClose.wav - "Truck Door Open and Close" by Jofae, https://freesound.org/people/Jofae/sounds/361823/ (CC0) - one recording split into the latch opening and the door shutting.
- TugHitchClunk.wav - "2 Heavy Metal Clangs Free" by AardsReal, https://freesound.org/people/AardsReal/sounds/842169/ (CC0) - the first clang: the towbar's eye dropping onto the hitch and the pin going in.
- TugHorn.wav - "Maquina de vapor .wav" by Camilo_Vargas13, https://freesound.org/people/Camilo_Vargas13/sounds/429834/ (CC0) - a tractor's horn, one blast.
- (also downloaded, kept for later: "FORD LTL 9000_engine start and idle.wav" by YellowJacketStudios, https://freesound.org/people/YellowJacketStudios/sounds/435656/ (CC0))
