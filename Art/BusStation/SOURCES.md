# Chablais bus station - references and licences

The model in this folder is our own work, built to the dimensions in `docs/Bus-Station-Plan.md`
(a procedural Blender script, `make_busstation.py`). No geometry, texture or file from any other
model was used or downloaded; the photographs below were looked at as a reference for the look only.

## Reference photographs

1. **Chur Busbahnhof** - `Chur_Busbahnhof_ext_2015_wide.jpg`, by Cayambe, **CC BY-SA 4.0**
   (Wikimedia Commons). This is *the* look named in the plan: a glazed barrel vault carried on
   white tubular steel arches, cable-braced, over an open bus deck with an island platform.
   Taken from it: the arch form and whiteness, the glazing diagrid between purlins, the cable
   bracing in the roof plane, the gutters running along the arch feet.

2. **Aylesbury bus station** - `Aylesbury_bus_station_3.JPG`, by Arriva436, **CC BY 3.0**
   (Wikimedia Commons). Looked at and **rejected** as the overall look (the plan says so).
   Only two details were kept from it: the coloured resin walking route across the deck and the
   disc luminaires. Neither is in this structure step - they belong to the deck finishes and
   lighting steps that come later.

## Textures

All textures in this folder are generated procedurally by `make_busstation.py` (numpy, written as
PNG next to the model). Nothing is downloaded, so no third-party texture licence applies.
