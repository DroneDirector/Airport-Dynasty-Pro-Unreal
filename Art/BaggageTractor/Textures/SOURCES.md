# Baggage tow tractor textures - photo-scanned materials from ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com), all CC0 (public domain), 2K JPG; the same sets as Art/PushbackTug/Textures and Art/GPU/Textures (copied 2026-11-21)
- Paint004 - body paint tinted GSE yellow (the tug's yellow), the cab + roof tinted white (the GPU's white), red/yellow small parts (https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Paint004)
- PaintedMetal016 - worn yellow/black chevron panels on the rear face (same family as the tug's chevrons) (https://ambientcg.com/view?id=PaintedMetal016)
- Metal027 - bare and dark steel: hitches, pins, hinges, axles, battery tray, mirror arms (https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Metal027)
- DiamondPlate008A - the battery deck lid and the kick step tread (https://ambientcg.com/view?id=DiamondPlate008A)
- Rubber004 - tyres (no modelled tread: the tread look is this texture), seat bellows, bumper band, pedal pads (https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Rubber004)
- Rubber001 - the cab floor mat (https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Rubber001)
- Plastic006 - dash, pedestal, trim, mirror housings, handles, battery case (https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Plastic006)
- Leather026 - the seat's black vinyl (https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Leather026)
- Plastic013A - copied with the set (GPU canopy material), not used on this model
Lenses, glass, mirror glass and the display are plain (emissive / glass) materials; in the game the glass uses the fire truck's glass material.
