# Hangar reference photos and brief

Owner's reference photos (the anchor images) for the player hangars (one real hangar, photographed 2026-11-11). They show the look and the
details to copy; the building is rebuilt as our own model and scaled to four sizes. Never show aircraft, helicopters or
people in hangar renders or in the game's hangar art taken from these.

## Folders
- `1-hall-and-doors/` (20): the main hall and its doors. Dark grey tapered portal frames; dark roof purlins with a
  grey liner; twin-strip LED high-bays, a ridge light strip and skylight panels; white insulated wall liner with
  horizontal girts and X-bracing in the end bays; multi-leaf telescoping sliding doors (steel frame with X-bracing
  inside) stacking into side pockets; overhead festoon cable loops; floor rails in a concrete threshold strip and a
  trench drain outside; polished dark grey concrete with yellow lines; white or silver vertically ribbed cladding, a
  deep door header fascia, an obstruction light on the ridge, an asphalt apron, sprinkler pipes. Equipment: tool chests,
  cabinets, shelving, jack stands, a tug, desks, extinguishers.
- `2-exterior-back-corner/` (7): the corner behind the hangar. The plain back and side walls; a two-storey office
  annex (fine silver corrugated cladding, large dark windows with blue-grey panels between them, dark grey steel
  canopies, downpipes, LED wall lights, the address number); a single-storey store bay with a roll-up door and a grey
  personnel door; asphalt with a concrete kerb and drain gratings; yellow bollards and a fire hydrant; a chain-link fence
  with barbed wire and a black screen; a sliding gate to the apron; a tall light pole with a camera; a planter with
  trees; a car park.
- `3-lobby-kitchen-corridor/` (4): the lobby (grey steel stairs with horizontal-bar railings up to a mezzanine,
  polished concrete, a wooden bench, a check-in desk, a direction-sign post, an AED); the kitchen on the upper floor
  (wood-veneer cabinets, white worktop, steel fridge, dishwasher, microwave, sink, coffee machines; open dark ceiling
  with spiral ducts and white pendant lamps; a steel double door); the corridor with wood-veneer toilet doors, a grey
  exit door, CCTV cameras, an extinguisher and a thermostat.
- `4-lounge-and-store/` (5): the crew lounge, a room of its own on the ground floor (black leather sofas and
  armchairs, rugs, slatted steel coffee tables, high tables with bar stools, wall TVs, plants, floor lamps, a mountain
  photo wall, wood-framed frosted glass doors, dark exposed steel joists and deck, spiral ducts, white pendant lamps,
  windows to the outside); the store bay entered from the hall through a roll-up door (shelving, a glass-door
  fridge, washing machines, an ice machine, carts, bins, a yellow gas-cylinder cage, a hand sink, a second roll-up door
  to outside).

## Brief
- Four hangar halls sized from real aircraft: small (A318 or Learjet), medium (A320), large (A321) and extra large
  (A380). Only the hall where the aircraft stands changes size.
- The annex rooms (lobby with stairs, corridor, toilets, crew lounge, kitchen upstairs, store bay) have the same
  layout in every size, placed as identical instances against the hall's side wall.
- The route to the lounge: from the hall, through a personnel door into the corridor, then along it to the lounge.
- The hall has two personnel doors on each side wall. Clicking one in the game takes you inside.
- The main door is shown both open and shut, including the parts that hold the leaves upright and the drive mechanism
  (bogies, motors, guides, festoon cables).
- The hall has its lamps, a glass rooflight along the ridge, and textures detailed down to the cables, switches and
  screws.
- Every hangar picture shows width, depth and height.
- Renders: first a plain overview of each size, then interiors, equipment close-ups and the rooms.
