# Baggage tow tractor (electric, closed cab) - research notes (2026-11-21)

Compact European battery-electric baggage tractor with a closed cab, the class that pulls 4-6 baggage carts / ULD
dollies on the apron (Charlatte T135 / T137, Mulag Comet 3E / 4E, TLD JST-E, Linde P250 class). 80 V traction
battery under the rear deck, AC motor on the rear axle, 15-30 kN drawbar pull, 20-30 km/h.

## What the real ones have in common

- **Body shape**: a low steel tub about 3.0-3.7 m long, 1.3-1.45 m wide, sill ~0.45-0.55 m up. Cab in the front
  half, rear deck in the back half. Two families: cab-forward with a flat, near-vertical front face (Charlatte
  T135/T137, Mulag 4E) or a short sloped bonnet ahead of the windscreen (Mulag Comet 3, TLD JST). The cab roof is
  flat, ~1.95-2.05 m above ground, a little overhanging; corner pillars are thin square tubes.
- **Wheels**: small (6.00-9 / 7.00-12 or 15-16 in) under deep square cut-outs, front axle steers, rear axle drives;
  wheelbase ~1.5-1.9 m, so short overhangs front and rear.
- **Cab door**: one door each side (or open sides). On the European closed cabs it is a **hinged door, hinged on its
  front edge** (Charlatte T135, Mulag Comet top view) - like a car door. TLD electrics use a **sliding door on a top
  rail** that parks over the rear body. Door glass: big upper pane, often a lower pane too; cheap cabs use a vinyl
  door.
- **Cab glass**: one large flat windscreen with a single wiper from the top; side glass in the door; a **rear
  window covered by a steel mesh guard** (protects against bags); some have a small fixed pane ahead of the door.
- **Interior**: one driver seat, central or just off-centre, sitting ~0.87-0.90 m above ground (seat-switch,
  suspension seat); steering wheel on a raked column coming out of a sloped dash pod in front of the seat; flat
  floor with a single low step at the door; accelerator + brake pedals on the floor (2 pedals, no clutch); forward/
  reverse switch and display on the dash; hand parking brake lever beside the seat; extinguisher on a bracket by
  the seat. A second (side) seat exists only on double-cab versions.
- **Batteries**: 80 V lead-acid tray (1.35-1.55 t) or Li-ion pack lying **under the rear deck behind the cab**,
  low between the axles/over the rear axle. Access: the deck is made of **chequer-plate lids that lift up**
  (Charlatte: two/three plates with a grab rail around them; Mulag: deck/tailgate panels), or the battery slides
  out sideways on rollers for swap. Charge socket (Rema plug) + emergency stop on the rear face or rear corner.
- **Hitches**: rear pin hitch jaw with a vertical pin on a chain, adjustable to 3 heights (T135: 280 / 400 / 490 mm);
  many also have a front hitch/tow eye in the bumper for positioning.
- **Lamps**: front - square or upright rectangular headlamp pods low in the front face at both corners, amber
  indicators beside/above them, black rubber bumper band below; rear - tail/brake/indicator/reverse clusters low on
  the rear face corners, often a light bar at the top of the cab back (work lights onto the hitch); one amber
  beacon on the roof (front or rear corner; T135 shows two on the front corners); reflectors round.
- **Mirrors**: two flat rectangular mirrors on short stalks from the top front corners of the cab / A-pillars.
- **Steps**: no separate ladder - the floor is low (~0.4-0.5 m), with a sill step at the door opening, often
  with a black anti-slip strip; grab handle on the pillar.

## What the model is

Built by Tools/make_baggage_tractor.py (Blender 3.3; shared helpers/checks Tools/vehicle_kit.py), our own design, cab-forward
like the T135 / Comet 4E: 3420 long with both hitches (body 2980), 1400 wide (2030 over the mirrors), roof 2000, beacon 2165,
wheelbase 1580, track 1120, front tyres 520 x 160 (6.00-9 class, steer +-32 deg in deep arches), rear 620 x 200 (7.00-12, driven),
floor 620, seat cushion 920 (T135: 865-898), deck/belt 1020 (T135 platform 1035), ground clearance 140.
Yellow body (tug paint), white cab + roof (GPU white), one hinged driver door on the left (front-edge hinges, as T135/Comet),
fixed glass elsewhere, rear window behind a mesh guard, rear deck = chequer-plate battery lid hinged at its rear edge over the
80 V battery, rear 3-height pin jaw (eye centres 280 / 400 / 490 as the T135 sheet; the GPU eye at 400 uses the middle slot),
front pin jaw at 400, emergency stop + charge socket on the rear face. Blueprint: BaggageTractor-Blueprint-v2.png.
