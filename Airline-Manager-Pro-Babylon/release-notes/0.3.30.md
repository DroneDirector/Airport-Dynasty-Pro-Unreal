# 0.3.30

## Real weather in the 3D viewer
- The sky follows the nearest real airport report where the aircraft is, refreshed every half minute.
- Cloud cover (few, scattered, broken, overcast), the cloud base and the depth of the layer come from the report; towering cumulus and thunderstorm clouds build tall.
- Heavy cloud and storms darken the light; the clouds drift with the reported wind.
- Below the cloud base: rain or snow round the camera, as hard as the report says.
- Thunderstorms: lightning from the cloud base to the ground, with its flash and thunder.
- The station and its report show at the top of the screen; the Real weather button turns it on or off.
