# 0.3.44

## The apron, further out
- The stands are 26 m (jetway gates) and 28 m (walk-out gates) out from the pier, with their lead-in lines, stop bars and PBB wheel boxes. The apron taxilane, the taxiways, the runway, the cargo stands and the airport's boundary moved out with them.
- A glazed fixed corridor runs from each of gates 1-4 to its rotunda, sloping 5° down from the gate floor and finished flush at both ends, with a 15 cm steel trim on the gate's glass.

## The jetways, rebuilt
- Three fixed tunnel sections (8.0, 7.6 and 7.2 m), each sliding inside the one before and always overlapping by at least 1.5 m. The tunnel telescopes from 8.0 to 19.8 m.
- The rotunda never moves: the jetway turns round it on a turning sleeve and tilts from it. Rubber bellows seal both ends all round, lined inside, and stretch as the jetway moves.
- The drive unit: two lift columns, a wide sleeve on the tunnel riding over a thinner leg on the base. A steered bogie with two pairs of wheels in tandem, with rounded tyres, a real tread and deep silver rims.
- Docking and undocking at real times: about 2 minutes to dock (drive, final crawl with the cab turning to the fuselage, canopy and doors) and about 1½ minutes to undock. The wheels steer and roll, and everything else follows them.
- The amber beacon turns at 75 flashes a minute, lighting the apron, only while the jetway moves.
- The cab: doors with small windows that open when docked, a side window, yellow padded bumpers, the operator's console with its joystick and screens, the canopy lined inside, and the gate's number on the roof, lit from dusk to just after sunrise.
- The service stair has wheels at its foot and guardrails that carry on round the landing to the jetway's side. Its steps stay level.
- Ribbed, swaying yellow air hoses hang in metal pipes under the rotunda and by the drive unit, bending round whatever they touch.

## Inside
- A brushed slatted ceiling with flush LED panels, a dark carpet, satin grey wall panels and polished chrome handrails.
- Yellow and black strips at every joint, and sliding floor plates between the sections.
- Exit signs, a fire extinguisher in its cabinet, vents, a camera dome, a service door with a push bar, and backlit advertising panels.
- Screens, advertising panels and exit signs glow softly, so they stay readable.

## Your own models and pictures
- Many jetway parts can be swapped for your own model, picture or video: the wheels, bogie, lift columns, cab front, console, canopy, rotunda drum and machinery, stair, beacon, fire extinguisher, the floors, bellows, cladding and ceilings, and the advertising panels. Put the file in `models/` (see its README). On every start the game checks each one and reports it in Settings → Updates and errors.

## Testing
- "Animate jetways (test)" in airport mode docks or undocks every jetway. It's a test button and will be removed before 1.0.
