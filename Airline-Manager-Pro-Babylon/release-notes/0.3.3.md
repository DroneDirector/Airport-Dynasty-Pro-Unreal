# Airport Dynasty Pro 0.3.3

**The 3D viewer follows the game's real flights.** Open **3D** on the map, or on a flight or an aircraft, in the game.

- The flight as the game flies it: along its schedule and flight plan, in real time.
  - Its real altitude, speed, heading, climb and descent.
  - It banks in the turns and pitches in the climbs and descents.
  - Contrails only at cruise height.
- Your own flights, your airline's and other airlines' flights.
  - The aircraft is painted in its airline's colours.
  - Other aircraft nearby (within 40 km) fly round it.
- **‹ ›** beside the callsign switch to your airline's next or previous flight. When a flight ends, the viewer moves
  to the next one.
- The panel shows the callsign, the route, the airline, the type and registration, the altitude (flight level or
  feet), speed, heading, climb or descent, phase and passengers.
- The sun, the moon and the stars are where they really are for the flight's position, at the real time.
- **Back to the game** (or Circle on a controller) goes back to the interface. The game keeps running behind the 3D
  viewer the whole time: the airline, its schedules and the other airlines' flights go on.
- All camera angles work: behind, side, front, above, below, the windows, the tail and fly-by.

**Coming next:** the ground under the flights (the airports, their runways and stands, and the terrain), and the
landing gear. Until then, aircraft on the ground are shown at sea level.
