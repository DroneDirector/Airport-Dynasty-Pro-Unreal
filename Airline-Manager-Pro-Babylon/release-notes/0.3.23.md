# 0.3.23 – Your own terminal at the airport site

- The airport site now has your real terminal in place of the stand-in shapes. It's built by the terminal's own builders and is the same as in the Terminal demo:
  - the Grand Hall and its wave roof, the entrances and their glass;
  - every room, wall, door, stair and lift;
  - the walkway under its glass barrel vault;
  - the gates with their glass walls, the furniture, screens, lamps and plants, and the museum;
  - its own grounds: the kerb, the road, the car park, the apron with the aircraft and jet bridges at the stands, and the rental car centre.
- It sits on the levelled ground at the airport's height, joined to the site's taxiways and runways. The stand-in shapes, their apron and the extra aircraft are gone.
- The Gate 4 view is inside the real gate 4 on the upper floor, at its glass wall, looking out over the apron and the runways to the mountains.
- The tower moved to the west side, clear of the rental car centre.
