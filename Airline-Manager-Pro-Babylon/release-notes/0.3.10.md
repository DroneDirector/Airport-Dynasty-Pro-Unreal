# Airport Dynasty Pro 0.3.10

**Your museum is now the Vortex Gallery.** It fully replaces the old museum, in the same place off the concourse's
west wall.

- **The hall:** a 30 × 20 m oval. Its white high-gloss walls sweep up into the ceiling as one shell, with 64 ribs
  following the curve and lines of light along them.
- **The oval skylight** at 9.2 m, with its glowing rim, and rings of light round the hall.
- **The gallery ribbon** at 4.4 m, with a glass balustrade and light along its edges.
- **A glossy floor** with lines of light flowing in from the entrance round the centrepiece.
- **The centrepiece:** an airliner in your colours hovering at 3.6 m over a dark round plinth with cyan rings and a
  beam of light.
- **Three curved display walls** with backlit niches, and pedestals 1–8 with glass domes and name plates. When your
  collection grows, more pedestals stand round the hall.
- **The entrance:** a portal on the concourse with a glowing double arch, the museum's name and "Vortex Gallery" over
  it, and glass doors that slide apart as you come up to them and shut behind you.
- The Collection list still takes you to any piece and puts it in your hands.
