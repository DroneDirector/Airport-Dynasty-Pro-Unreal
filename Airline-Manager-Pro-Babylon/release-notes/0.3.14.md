# Airport Dynasty Pro 0.3.14

**The terminal's walkway to the gates is now 16 m wide (it was 10 m).**

- The two moving walkways run side by side down the middle, east and west, with one rail between them and arrows
  on them.
- That leaves a clear lane about 6 m wide on each side, from one gate to the next.
- A seating island sits in each crossing between the walkways' sections, and planters stand along the south wall.
- The baggage hall under the walkway is 16 m wide too, so the floors line up. Its sorting belts and baggage carts
  have more room.
- The gates, view bays and ramp services on both floors have moved 6 m north to make room, with their stairs,
  lifts, screens and doors. The stands, the aircraft and jetways J-1 to J-4 have moved with them.
