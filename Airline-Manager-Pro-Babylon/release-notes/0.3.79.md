# 0.3.79

## Two new shadow settings (Settings → Graphics)
- Shadow distance: a slider from 30 m to All. Things further away from you cast no shadow. Fewer casters means less work, and the shadow map covers less ground, so the shadows near you get sharper. The default is 150 m on phones and tablets and All on the PC.
- Shadow updates: every frame, every 2nd frame or every 3rd frame. Drawing the shadows less often is faster. Shadows of moving things (people, jetways, the sun) may trail a little behind. The default is every frame.
- Both apply at once, in the airport too.
