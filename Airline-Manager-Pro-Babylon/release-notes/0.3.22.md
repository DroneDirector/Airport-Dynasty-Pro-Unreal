# 0.3.22 – Level ground under the airport, anywhere in the world

- Anything placed in the world can now level the ground under it. Each placed model gets a flat zone: its footprint (a rectangle turned with it, or a circle), the height it sits at, and a smooth slope back to the natural ground around it. Every terrain in the game builds its ground through these zones, so it works wherever in the world a model is put. That covers the airport site and the 3D viewer's ground.
- When a model is placed later, the ground under it is laid out again. The height can be given, or taken from the middle of the ground's own heights under the model.
- Airport site: the fenced area and the road in are flat zones at the airport's height, so the runways, taxiways, apron and buildings sit flush on the ground, with the land sloping smoothly back to its own height around them.
- Airport site: finer depth precision across its 120 km view, and the runways, taxiways, apron and their markings are drawn just above the ground. The ground no longer flickers through them at a distance.
