# Airport Dynasty Pro 0.3.7

**The joined building, on Babylon.js**, to the main game's plan. You walk through all of it.

- **Your office** and **the Operations office** open off the **staff corridor**, each with an airport-style sign
  beside its door.
- At the corridor's end, the staff door opens onto **the concourse**:
  - a long hall with a stone floor and a glass wall onto the apron, with aircraft at the gates;
  - four shops along the other wall, rows of seats by the glass, pillars with plants;
  - the Departures and Arrivals boards hanging before the lounge.
- At the concourse's far end, **the Business lounge**: its sliding doors open as you come up to them and shut behind
  you.
- **Every door** opens and shuts with a tap or a click (E or Cross in the middle of the view).
- **Every room works as it does on its own:**
  - in your office, the laptop, monitor, route map and reports open the game's screens;
  - in Operations, the curved monitor shows your live schedule and the buttons open Operations, the Schedule, Dispatch
    and the Flight planner;
  - the chairs sit you down;
  - in the lounge, the fire burns and the boards update.
- The panel at the top shows where you are, and **Back to the game** takes you back.

**Getting in**, from the game:

- **Office** brings you into your office, **Operations** to the Operations desk, and **Lounges** to just inside the
  lounge's doors.
- **Airport**, new in the menu, brings you into the concourse.
- You always arrive standing on open floor: never inside a wall, a seat or a counter.
