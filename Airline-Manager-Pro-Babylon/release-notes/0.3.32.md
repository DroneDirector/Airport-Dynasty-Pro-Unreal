# 0.3.32

## The Training room (the Academy)
- A classroom off the staff corridor, opposite Operations, through double doors that open inward; a sign in the corridor.
- The whiteboard shows today's course and the lesson as it goes; the instructor's desk and chair, the instructor at the board.
- 12 desks in three rows of four with an aisle down the middle, each with its chair, the airline's Academy notebook, pens, and calculators and paper on some.
- Bookshelves with aviation books, two posters, a clock with moving hands, plants and windows.
- The class: everyone of yours on a course sits at a desk. Tap someone to see who they are, their course and how long is left; tap the instructor for what he is saying.
- The lesson runs line by line on the board and under the screen, with a voice you can turn on.
- Go to the Training room (Crew → Training) opens it.
