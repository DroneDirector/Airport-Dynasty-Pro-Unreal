# 0.3.73

## Texture arrays and shared materials
- When the airport opens, every fixed object's material is looked at. Materials that are the same in everything but their colour and their picture now become one: the colour moves into the object itself, and the pictures go into texture arrays (stacks of same-sized pictures, one stack per size). Pictures that repeat across a surface still repeat cleanly, as each layer keeps its own edges.
- The airport goes from 703 materials to 549. Fewer materials let more objects be merged into one draw call: about 2 to 3% fewer draw calls.
- Everything else about each material stays exactly as it was: reflections, see-through, refraction, clear coat, lighting and shadows. The picture looks the same.
- Glowing pictures (signs), cut-out pictures, screens, clocks, the lift displays, anything that moves or opens, swappable models and the wet ground and puddles keep their own materials.
- Anything added to the game later is included on its own when the airport opens: new pictures join an array of their size, or start a new one.
- Needs WebGL2 or WebGPU. Phones and PCs without them keep the separate pictures.
