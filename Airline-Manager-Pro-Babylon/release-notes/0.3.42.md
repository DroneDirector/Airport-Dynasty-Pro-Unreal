# 0.3.42

## New jetways at gates 1–4
- Every jetway is the same new apron-drive boarding bridge, 18.5 m long and 4.2 m high:
  - a glazed vestibule on the gate's jetway door, its floor flush with the gate floor (+6.00);
  - the round rotunda (3.6 m) on its two columns, with its machinery, service cabinet and yellow air hoses;
  - the glazed telescopic tunnel in three sections, its walkway 2.5 m wide, with the steel X truss outside the glass;
  - the drive unit: lift columns on a two-wheel bogie, with air hoses;
  - the service stair down to the apron;
  - the round rotating cab, with the operator's console, the bumper and the folding canopy at the aircraft's front door.
- Inside: an anti-slip floor, handrails on both sides, ceiling lights and windows all along. You can walk from the gate through the jetway down to the aircraft door.
- Only the slope changes: the tunnel runs from the gate floor down to the door sill (3.3 m).
- The drive wheels stand on the PBB wheel box painted on the apron, and the rotunda's keep-clear circle is painted where the rotunda stands.
