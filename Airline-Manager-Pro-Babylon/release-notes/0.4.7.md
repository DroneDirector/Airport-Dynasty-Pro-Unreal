# 0.4.7

## Your airport's control tower
- In airport mode (PC), the control tower now stands at the west end of the apron, beside the pier, in the same white cladding, timber and glass as the terminal. It replaces the old simple tower behind the terminal.
- **Walk right in:** through the glass doors at its foot (they slide open as you come to them), take the lift up to the lift lobby, then the spiral stair into the cab.
- **The cab:** glass all round, leaning out at the top, with one unbroken ring of control desks, 24 positions with their screens, and 12 chairs.
- **The balcony:** walk up to the desk by the balcony door and its flap lifts; the door swings out onto the walkway round the cab.
- Everything in the tower is solid: walls, glass, desks, chairs, the lift and the railing.

## Quick places in the holographic map
- The "Go to" places are now in the full-screen holographic map, along its bottom: the terminal's (Entrance, Main hall, Gate 4 and the rest) and the control tower's (the cab looking out over the apron, the balcony, and the ground at its entrance). Jump, Crouch, Prone and Sprint stay in the lower right.

## Lifts
- The lifts can now serve any two levels at their own speed, so the tower's lift runs from the ground to the lift lobby and shows G and ATC.
