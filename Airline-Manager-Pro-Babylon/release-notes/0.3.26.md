# 0.3.26 – Sprint, crouch, jump and prone

- In airport mode (Airport in the game's menu), walking now has stances and moves:
  - Sprint: hold Shift, or use the Sprint button, which stays on until you press it again. It's about 6 m/s going forward, slower when crouched or prone.
  - Crouch: C or Ctrl, or the button. Your eyes drop to about 1 m, you move slower, and you fit under lower things.
  - Prone: Z, or the button. You lie flat with your eyes about 30 cm off the floor and crawl slowly.
  - Jump: Space, or the button. About a metre up, only from the ground. From a crouch or lying down it stands you up first.
- Pressing Crouch or Prone again stands you back up, if there's room above you. If there isn't, you're told so and stay down.
- The stances change your body's height for bumping into things, so you really fit under what you crouch or crawl beneath.
