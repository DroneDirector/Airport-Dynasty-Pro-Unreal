# 0.3.58

## Faster walking, above all on phones
- **Walking into walls:** the buildings' walls and floors, the jetways and the aircraft each get an invisible stand-in cut into small pieces, so walking only checks the pieces near you, not whole buildings. This was the likely cause of the frame rate falling to 1 while walking. It costs no extra drawing.
- **Eye adaptation:** its check for a roof overhead now looks only at big things (buildings, jetways), not all 15,000 objects.
- **The airport map on phones and tablets:** drawn without its glow blur (very slow on phones) and 10 times a second instead of 20. It looks almost the same.

## The map's place
- In airport mode the map no longer covers Exit, the gear, Services and concessions, Weather or Photo. When something else is where it would go, it sits on the left, below the panels there.

## See where the time goes
- The CPU line of the performance meter (Settings → Graphics) now shows what takes the processor's time each frame, part by part: walking, jetways, weather, effects, the map, doors and lifts, the airfield and drawing.
