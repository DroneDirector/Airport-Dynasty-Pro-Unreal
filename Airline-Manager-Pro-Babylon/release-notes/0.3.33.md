# 0.3.33

## Your pet in your office
- Your dog or cat (chosen and made your own in Your pilot → Pet) sleeps in its bed by the bookshelf, its two bowls beside it.
- Our own sculpted model, covered in thousands of strands of fur, in your pet's coat, markings, size and ears.
- It sleeps curled up, breathing slowly, an ear twitching now and then, paws twitching in a dream (a dog) or the tail tip flicking (a cat).
- Now and then it wakes, gets up, walks about the office, has a drink or a bite, comes back, turns round in its bed and lies down again.
- Tap it and it wakes for a moment; tap a bowl to fill it up (the water pours in, the kibble rains in). How full the bowls are is saved.
- The food bowl has your pet's name on it, the other says WATER.
- The pet editor's picture (Your pilot → Pet) draws again: your pet as it is now, in its bed.
