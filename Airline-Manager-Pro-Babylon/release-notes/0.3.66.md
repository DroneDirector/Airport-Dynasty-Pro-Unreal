# 0.3.66

## Lens flares fixed
- The sun's lens flares no longer put big discs across the middle of the view. The ghosts are now small and faint.
- They only show while the sun is up and you can see it through the clouds, and they fade as clouds cover it. Only big solid things (buildings, aircraft, jetways) hide them. Glass doesn't.

## Airport loads faster
- While the airport is built, materials are no longer re-checked after every single change. They are all checked once, just before the graphics are prepared. This saves processor time while loading, on phones most of all. Nothing changes in how it looks.
