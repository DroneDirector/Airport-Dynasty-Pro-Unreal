## Puddles like the real thing
- **Puddles in sheets of water:** they are broad and shallow, with ragged edges, and lie in the hollows of the apron, the taxiways and the roads. On the runway they are drawn out along it, in the wheel ruts. A damp sheen comes first, then the water spreads the longer it rains, and it dries from the edges after.
- **Real reflections:** the puddles mirror what stands round them: the terminal, the jetways, the light masts, the aircraft and the sky. The reflection is stronger towards the horizon, as on real water, and the raindrops ripple it. How sharp it is follows the Reflections setting in Settings → Graphics; with it off, the puddles still reflect the sky.
- **Every paved surface outside:** the road, the kerb and the paths behind the terminal, where people walk in, and the stands get the same wet ground and puddles as the airfield. The markings get wet too.

## Skid marks on the runway
- New skid marks in the two touchdown zones: dark rubber in the wheel tracks, heavy where the tyres touch and fading the way the aircraft rolls, with a light trace along the whole runway. They come from a seamless picture that tiles along the runway, so no repeats or seams show.
