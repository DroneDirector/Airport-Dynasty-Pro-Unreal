# 0.3.34

## Crash sites in the 3D viewer
- After a crash, the airport keeps the site for three days, at the airports of the flight you follow.
- The aircraft broken in three (nose, the middle with the wings, the tail), burnt, lying where it came to rest beyond the runway.
- Wreckage strewn back along its path, burnt ground and the scar where it slid.
- Fires for the first six hours (their glow on the ground at night), smoke drifting off for more than a day.
- Fire engines, a command vehicle and ambulances round it, their lights flashing for the first twelve hours.
- An aircraft only damaged lies in one piece, down on a broken gear leg, with the parts that came off near by.
- A crash on the map or in a notification opens the 3D viewer at that airport, looking at the wreck (drag to look round, zoom in and out).
