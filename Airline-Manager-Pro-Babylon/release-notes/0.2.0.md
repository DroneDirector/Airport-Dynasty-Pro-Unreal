# Airport Dynasty Pro 0.2.0

**3D viewer (demo)**, a new button on the title screen. It goes straight into a flight at flight level 300
(30,000 ft), from Vágar to Copenhagen along its flight plan.

- **Volumetric clouds** below the aircraft: real 3D cloud fields with their own shade, bright edges towards the sun
  and the colour of the hour. The sea is under them on the curve of the Earth, with the sun's glitter and the haze of
  the distance.
- **Time of day**: the sun rises and sets, the moon follows with its real phase, and the real stars (from the
  Hipparcos catalogue) turn through the night, all where they really are for the aircraft's position and the time.
  The flight opens above the clouds at sunset.
- **Time slider (demo only)**: drag it to move the sun at once and see the light change on the clouds, the sky and
  the aircraft. Time speed: pause, 1×, 60× or 600×.
- **The aircraft**: a narrow-body twin jet of the A320 family's size, built from its published dimensions, in our own
  livery. Gloss paint reflects the sky, the engine fans turn, contrails stream behind, and the lights work:
  navigation, strobes, beacons, and logo lights on the fin at night.
- **Camera angles**: behind, side, front, above, below, left window, right window, tail and fly-by.
  - Drag, the mouse or the right stick looks round.
  - Pinch, the wheel or the left stick zooms.
  - Keys 1–9 or L1/R1 change the camera; the D-pad left and right moves the sun.

**In every place**

- The gear, Exit and the new **Title screen** button are always on screen. Title screen takes you back to the start
  screen. Circle on a controller does the same.

**Settings**

- New **Volumetric clouds** setting (Low to Ultra), set with each quality preset.
- Reflections now cover the aircraft's paint too.
