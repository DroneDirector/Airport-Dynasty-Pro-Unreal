# 0.4.6

## The full-screen holographic map
- Following a flight in 3D, tap the map in the corner: it opens full screen with the whole world's coastlines, the land in holographic blue and the aircraft where it is. Drag to look round, pinch or use the wheel (or − and +) to zoom, Centre to follow the aircraft again, ✕ or Esc to close. Close to the aircraft it shows the same detailed ground as the corner map.

## Walking in the world: a sharper, greener world
- **Sharper ground:** close round you (about 12 km across) the heights and the satellite picture are four times finer, so mountains have their real shapes and sizes.
- **Ground detail:** a fine grain on the ground close up, so it isn't a blur.
- **Grass and trees:** grass where the ground is green and trees where there are woods, worked out from the satellite picture itself. New settings under Graphics: **Grass** and **Trees** (all the way down: none at all) and **Ground detail**.

## You can see where it rains and snows
- In the 3D view, rain and snow hang from the clouds to the ground as curtains you can see from far off, wherever a real airport report round about says it is falling (up to 16 places within about 160 km). Snow is paler and softer; heavier showers are wider and denser. Where nothing is falling, nothing is drawn.

## VIP charters are really flown
- Accepting a VIP charter now puts the trip there and the trip home on your **Jobs** board. Fly them yourself or give them to a hired pilot; the money comes as each flight reaches the gate, and the group's verdict and the newspapers come after the flights, good or bad.

## Air traffic control for the AI
- Every hired and AI flight talks to the airports it uses: Delivery, Ground, Tower, Departure, Control, Approach and Ground again, with the runway in use, the wind and its departure and arrival. Only one flight at a time is cleared on a runway. The newest calls are under **Radio** in each airport's view. Your airport has its own tower: "Mýrdalssandur Tower".

## Your airport on the map, with its own procedures
- **Mýrdalssandur (BIAP / VTX)** now shows on the 2D world map with its code and layout.
- It has its own made-up procedures that flights fly like real ones: departures (KATLA1D, SKOGR1D, …), arrivals (KATLA1A, ALDAN1A, …) and an ILS for each runway with its own fixes (ESKRA and FLAGA for runway 26, DYRHO and REYNI for 08). Routes to and from the north keep clear of the ice cap: departures climb straight ahead to 5,000 ft before turning, arrivals come in over the sands at 5,000 ft.
