# 0.3.67

## Airport: much less work each frame picking what is in view
- The airport has close to 15,000 objects. Until now, every one of them was tested on its own, every frame, to see whether it is in view.
- Now the 13,600 objects that never move are sorted into blocks of space, and whole blocks out of view are skipped at once. Everything that moves (people, vehicles, aircraft, doors, lifts, jetways) is still tested on its own every frame.
- The picture is exactly the same: the same objects are drawn as before. In our test a frame took about 36 ms instead of 64 ms. Phones and slower PCs gain the most.
