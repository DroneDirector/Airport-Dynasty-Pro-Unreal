# 0.3.78

## Walking: far less work for the processor
- Each step on foot was tested against nearly every solid thing in the airport, twice a frame (the step, then the fall). Now it is tested only against what is near you (within 20 m), and that list is kept fresh four times a second. Jumping to a place with Go to makes everything there solid at once.
- Walking feels exactly the same: the same walls, floors, stairs, seats and doors.

## Jetways: the hoses take less work
- The jetways' hoses sway in the wind as before. They are now worked out 30 times a second instead of every frame, not at all while the jetway is out of view, and the places they hang from and push against are worked out again only while the jetway moves.
- Docking, undocking and the swaying look the same.
