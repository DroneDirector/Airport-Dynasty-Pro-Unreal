# 0.3.35

## Car rental (Airport → Services and concessions)
- A new Services page (in the menu, and from the airport) with the rental companies of the rental car centre.
- Six companies (budget, standard, premium and electric), each with its own sedans, station cars, jeeps and sports cars, day rates, deposits, insurance excess and extras: zero excess, gravel protection, sand and ash protection, an extra driver, GPS, Wi-Fi, child seats.
- Sign a company's concession on one of three terms (standard, low rent with a higher share, high rent with a lower share): it pays you a share of what it takes and a monthly rent for its desk and bays. End it whenever you like.
- Passengers rent cars on the real clock: a full rental agreement each (the renter, the car and its plate, the dates, the rate, the extras, the deposit held).
- When a car comes back it is checked against the return terms (59 minutes' grace, late hours, fuel or charge, kilometres, cleaning, stone chips, damage up to the excess) and the final bill made; your share is paid in.
- Every company's rental and return terms, set out in full; your income, month by month.
- The rental car centre shows the same: only the companies signed at your airport have cars there, and a car that is out is not in its bay.
