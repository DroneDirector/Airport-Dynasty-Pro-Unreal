# Airport Dynasty Pro 0.3.12

**The new passenger terminal (concept 1), built to the architect's drawings (sheets A-01 to A-04).** Try it from the
title screen: **Terminal**, at the bottom of the menu with the other demos. You start in the Grand Hall, just inside
the main entrance. The gear (options), Title screen and X (quit) work as in the other demos.

- **Floor 1:**
  - the kerb under the wave roof, and the three entrances (revolving and sliding doors);
  - the Grand Hall, with check-in desks 1–24 on two islands, the eight bag drop points, and the trees in their
    white planters;
  - the museum (the Vortex Gallery), the plant room, toilets and the cleaners' store;
  - security with 6 lanes, then the airside lobby with stairs S-01 and lift E-01 to Floor 2;
  - the lounge, with copper waves, fireplace 7 and the black and brass L-shaped bar;
  - the staff wing as drawn: offices 1–4, Operations with its monitor wall, the crew room, staff toilets, the
    staff corridor and passage, the Academy (20 student desks and the teacher's desk), and the staff entrance with
    the stairs up to the bar;
  - the baggage hall under the walkway, ramp services 1–4, and walk-out gates 5–8.
- **Floor 2:**
  - the landing, then Tax Free;
  - the Food Walk with eateries 1–5, and the link to the walkway;
  - toilets, the family room, and the big bar with its oval counter;
  - the walkway to the gates, with moving walkways that carry you;
  - jetway gates 1–4 with jetways J-1 to J-4, and view bays 1–4;
  - the Grand Hall's open void below, under the wave roof.
- **The building:**
  - the wave roof (white on top, warm timber underneath, three lens skylights along its crests, downlights) on
    its eight tree columns C-1 to C-8;
  - the pier with solar panels on its roof;
  - outside: the road, the car park, the trees, and the apron with an aircraft on each of stands 1–8.
- **Everything has its id from the schedule:** 56 rooms, the doors, the stairs, the lifts, the timetable screens,
  the stands, the jetways and the columns.
  - Every object is its own part in the model, named with its room and kind, e.g. `F1-CHECKIN/DESK-07` or
    `F1-LOUNGE/FIREPLACE-7`. That covers each door, chair, table, lamp, plant, screen, desk and stool.
  - Each room's floor, walls and ceiling are the room's own.
  - The top of the screen shows where you are. Point at something on the PC to see its id.
- **Getting about:**
  - walk up and down the stairs;
  - tap or click a lift to change floor;
  - doors open as you come up to them;
  - you can walk out onto the kerb and the apron.
