# 0.3.16 – A glass vault over the walkway

- The long walkway to the gates has a glass barrel vault over its moving walkways: 10 m wide, on white steel ribs every 3 m with five purlins, rising to 9.65 m above the floor, with glass gables at both ends.
- Either side of it, flat white ceilings over the lanes sit right on top of the walkway's side walls, with panel lights along them.
- The vault's glass is real see-through glass with reflections, its own material with its own settings, so its look can be changed in one place.
- The walkway's ceiling is its own design that can be swapped for another; nothing else in the terminal depends on it.
- The solar panels on the roof keep clear of the vault.
