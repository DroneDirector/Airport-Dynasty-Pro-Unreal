# 0.3.94

## Holographic map: new navigation features (PC, airport mode)
- **One-tap destinations:** the full-screen map now has buttons for the main places (gates, security, check-in, lounges, baggage, toilets and the like). Tap one and the glowing route line takes you there, across floors.
- **Which floor:** when your waypoint is on another floor, the map shows ▲ FLOOR 2 or ▼ FLOOR 1 next to it.
- **Zooms with your speed:** the corner map stays close while you walk and zooms out as you run, so you see further ahead.
- Still there: the map turns with you, the route line with its stairs and lift tags, and the pulsing waypoint.
