# 0.4.8

## The hangar apron
- A new apron west of the cargo apron, in line with it, for hangars. Taxilane K now runs on west along it, and a new link, Q0, joins it to taxiway B at its west end.
- Three medium hangars stand on it, each sized for an Airbus A320 with room to walk round. Each has its yellow lead-in line from taxilane K straight to its door, and the line carries on into the hall.
- The airport map shows the new apron, the hangars with their crew buildings, and the lead-in lines.
- The detailed hangars, with their halls, doors, lamps and crew rooms, are in airport mode on the PC.
