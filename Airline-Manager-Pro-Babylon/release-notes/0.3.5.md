# Airport Dynasty Pro 0.3.5

**Your office, on Babylon.js.** In the game, open **Office** in the menu.

- **A warm, panelled corner office** to the main game's plan, 6.4 × 6.4 m, with daylight through the big window over
  the airport.
- **The desk:** a walnut executive desk with a green leather top and brass handles.
  - Your **laptop** shows your airline at a glance (balance, aircraft in the air, fleet, hub).
  - The **monitor** shows the flight planner.
  - A banker's lamp with its warm light, the phone, a notepad, coffee and the conduct reports.
  - Your **nameplate**, and a **model of one of your aircraft** in your company colours.
- **The room:**
  - bookshelves with books and trophies;
  - your **route map** with every route of your network;
  - two aviation posters and a propeller on the wall;
  - a leather sofa with a low table, and a globe.
- **Using things:** walk with W A S D, the stick or a controller, and look round.
  - Tap or click things (E or Cross in the middle of the view); their name shows as you look at them.
  - **Your chair**: sit down, and use it again to stand up.
  - **The laptop** opens the game at the Overview, the **monitor** the Flight planner, the **route map** your
    routes, the **reports** your crew.
  - **The door** goes back to the game.
- Every graphics setting applies here: shadows, reflections, glow, bloom, ambient occlusion and the rest.

**The lounges** in the game (Lounges in the menu, or a lounge's visit button) open the Business lounge on
Babylon.js. Its doors lead back to the game.

**Coming later:** inviting a crew member in over a conduct report (they walk in and sit down across the desk), with
the character animations.
