# 0.3.19 – Airport site

- A new demo on the title screen, Airport site, shows where the custom airport could stand in the real world, at real scale. It opens at Mýrdalssandur in south Iceland, and one button takes you to the Matanuska Valley in Alaska.
- Real terrain from the real heights, without satellite pictures. The detail is fine for 9 km in every direction round the airport and coarser out to about 36 km, on the curve of the Earth. The ground is coloured by height and slope: black sand or fields low down, moss and grass, scree, rock on the steep faces, and snow and ice up high. The sea, the sky, high clouds and a light haze in the distance complete it.
- The airport as planned, levelled into the ground:
  - the terminal and its pier, with glass along the gates;
  - four jet bridges, with eight aircraft on the stands;
  - the apron and the parallel taxiway, with links out to the runway;
  - two parallel 3,500 m runways 2,000 m apart, with markings and numbers, and taxiways crossing between them;
  - a tower, a hangar, a fire station, the access road, and the fence round about 21 km².
- The site's numbers are shown at the top: position, runway headings, the direction the gates face, and the ground height.
- Ready-made views: from a gate, on the apron, at the runway threshold, between the runways, from the air, and a low fly-by.
- A free drone camera with three speeds (Walk, Fast, Plane), plus the weather, time of day and Photo buttons from the Terminal demo. It's set to daytime, with good visibility, to start.
