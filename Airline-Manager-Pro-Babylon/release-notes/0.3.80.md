# 0.3.80

## New glass on the outside of the terminal
- The outer glazing has new glass: the main entrance and the Grand Hall's front, the gates' and the pier's windows onto the apron, and the jetways. It is our own seamless texture: the blue-green tint of coated airport glass, the gentle waviness of float glass, a light film of dust, small dried water spots and thin rain runs.
- Seen from outside, it works like real terminal glass: its coating mirrors the sky and clouds, stronger at an angle, and the inside is dimmed and harder to make out.
- From inside the terminal or a jetway, the reflections and most of the tint drop away, and you see out clearly. It changes as you go in or out.
- Glass inside the building, between rooms, stays as it was.
- The texture is 1024 px on the PC and 512 px on phones and tablets, compressed when Compressed textures is on. It adds no draw calls.
