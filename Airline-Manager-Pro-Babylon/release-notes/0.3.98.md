# 0.3.98

## Walk anywhere in the world
- A new button with a walking man sits right under the **3D** button on the world map (on the PC, above your airport button).
- Tap it and an animated X appears in the middle of the map. Move and zoom the map under it; the X stays the same size and marks where you'll stand. Tap the walking man again to cancel.
- Tap **Place in world** at the bottom of the map: a loading screen ("Placing avatar in world") stays until the ground round you and its satellite pictures are fully loaded, then you stand there and can walk freely (W A S D, Shift to run, the stick or a controller). **Back to the map** returns you to the map.
- On Android and the PC.

## Your airport button is PC only
- The button that takes the map to your airport is now on the PC only.

## The holographic map out in the world
- While you walk in the world, the holographic map sits in the bottom-left corner, as in the airport. For now it shows its grid round you; more will be drawn on it later.

## Walking in the world: only what you need
- Nothing from your airport's walk-in mode shows out in the world. On screen: **Back to the overview** in the top-left corner, the holographic map in the bottom-left, and **Jump, Crouch, Prone and Run** in the bottom-right.
- Crouching and lying prone lower your view smoothly and slow you down; a jump rises about a metre and lands. Keys: Space jump, C crouch, Z prone, Shift run.

## Cinema and Hide UI while walking in the world
- **Cinema** (top left): photo mode, as in the 3D viewer, with its looks, wide frame, depth of field and caption, plus **● Record video** at the top. Recording runs until you tap Stop (at most two minutes); videos are saved to Movies/Airport Dynasty Pro on Android and Pictures\Airport Dynasty Pro on the PC.
- **Hide UI** (top left): everything on the screen goes away. Double-tap the screen (or double-click) to bring it back; on the PC, Esc works too.
