# 0.3.36

## Lifts in airport mode
- Every lift (E-01 and the gate lifts E-G1–4) now has its own shaft through both floors, clad outside, with the hole cut in Floor 2's slab and the ceilings for it; the shaft is a touch wider than the hole, so its walls cover the slab's edge: no gaps, nothing to see between the floors, and the car never passes through anything.
- A proper car: a floor, brushed steel walls with a mirror, a handrail, a lit ceiling, the button panel and a display showing the floor and the way it is going.
- Doors on each floor and the car's own: two leaves sliding apart behind the front panels, opening and closing together.
- It works as a lift does: come up to the doors and it is called to your floor and opens; step in, and after a moment the doors close and it takes you to the other floor, speeding up and slowing down gently, then opens again. E (or X / Square on a controller) does it at once.
- In the terminal demo the lift is at your floor with its doors open when you arrive.

## A controller in airport mode
- A / Cross jumps, B / Circle crouches, Y / Triangle lies prone, X / Square uses a lift, R3 sprints (L3 runs while held, as before).
