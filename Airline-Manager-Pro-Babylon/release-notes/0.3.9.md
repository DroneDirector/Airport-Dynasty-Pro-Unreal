# Airport Dynasty Pro 0.3.9

**The water salute, on Babylon.js.** The first time one of your aircraft lands at an airport it has never been to,
the fire service welcomes it.

- **Two fire trucks** stand either side of the taxiway, their blue lights flashing.
- **Their roof cannons spray water** in an arch about 24 m high. The droplets fly on their real arcs under gravity,
  with spray and mist where they come down.
- **Your aircraft**, in your colours with its gear down and its lights on, taxis underneath and then comes round again.
- **Watch again** starts it over.
- **Camera angles:** side, under the arch, behind, from a truck, and high above. Drag to look round, and zoom with
  pinch, the wheel or the left stick.
- **The sky, the sun and the light** are that airport's own, now: by day, at sunset or at night under the stars,
  with the taxi lights on after dusk.
- Opened from the game: the news article's 3D button, or as it happens.
