# Airport Dynasty Pro 0.3.8

**Your museum, in the joined building.** It opens off the concourse's west wall, through its own animated double
doors, under a **Museum** sign. A **Your collection** sign stands beside them.

- **Everything you and your airline have been given or earned is on display**, each piece on its own plinth with a
  ring of light and a brass name plate:
  - the gifts from the airports (aircraft models in your colours, plaques, vases, dishes, tins, books, carvings and
    framed pictures);
  - your career awards (certificates and medals);
  - your best badges, as medals in their tier's metal (bronze, silver, gold, platinum, diamond, legendary).
- **Take a piece in your hands**: tap it (or E / Cross in front of it). It turns slowly in front of you; tap again to
  put it back.
- **The Collection menu** (top right, everywhere in the building) lists everything you have acquired over time. Tap
  one and you are taken to it, facing it, with it in your hands.
- **The hall** is as big as your collection needs: a polished dark floor, light strips, your museum's name over the
  doors and a strip of your company colour along the walls.
- **Getting in:** the museum in the game opens the building in the museum, standing on open floor.
- The museum is placed where nothing else stands, so no room overlaps another.
