# 0.3.52

## Navigation on the airport map
- **Navigate:** the button under the corner map (or M, or a tap on the map) opens the map full screen. It pops out from the corner with a hologram's flicker, and **Minimise** (or M or Esc) shrinks it back the same way.
- **Set a waypoint:** choose a floor and tap where you want to go. The waypoint is set and the map minimises, so you can start walking. Drag to move the map; scroll or pinch to zoom. **Centre on me** brings it back to you.
- **The route:** a warm, flowing line from where you are, through the doorways, up or down the stairs or the lift, to the waypoint. It takes the shortest way, staff doors and staff stairs included (you're the manager), and follows you as you walk.
- **Stairs and lifts:** the route marks them, for example "▲ LIFT UP · FLOOR 2" or "▼ STAIRS DOWN · FLOOR 1".
- **Floors:** the route is bright on your floor and only a faint ghost on the others, and it switches by itself when you change floor, like the map.
- **Distance and arriving:** the corner map shows where you're heading and how far it is. Arriving clears the waypoint. ✕ under the map, or "Remove waypoint" on the full map, removes it any time.
