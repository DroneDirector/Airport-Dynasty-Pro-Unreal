# 0.3.65

## The game is now called Airport Dynasty Pro
- The start screen shows the new name with the same neon animation, and the name is changed everywhere in the game, the installer and the phone app.
- Your settings and saves stay where they are. On the PC the update replaces the old shortcuts with new ones.

## Airport mode: quick viewpoints
- Airport mode now starts out on the apron beside gate 4's jetway and its stair, with the aircraft on your right.
- Two new buttons next to Jump, Crouch, Prone and Sprint take you straight there: View 1 (gate 4's jetway) and View 2 (on the apron, looking along the terminal to the jetways).
