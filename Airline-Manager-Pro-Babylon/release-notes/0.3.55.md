# 0.3.55

## Faster airport, same look
- **Glow:** the glow on lights, signs and screens is now worked out only from the things that glow and the big things that stand in front of them (walls, jetways, aircraft). It looks the same, with far fewer draw calls.
- **Light rays:** only big things (buildings, jetways, aircraft, masts) are checked for blocking the sun's rays. Small things never showed in the rays anyway.
- **Puddle reflections:** the puddles reflect what can actually show in them: buildings, jetways, masts, aircraft and the sky. They no longer redraw the whole terminal's furniture upside down (about 2,700 objects instead of 15,000).
- **The airline in the background:** while you're in a 3D place, the game's interface is fully hidden, so it doesn't draw its screens or poll controllers behind the 3D. Your airline keeps running as before, and the office monitors keep updating.
- In the test airport this cuts the draw calls by about a third on the lighter settings, and by more on High and Ultra.
