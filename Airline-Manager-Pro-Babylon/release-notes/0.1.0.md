# Airport Dynasty Pro 0.1.0

The first look at Airport Dynasty Pro on the Babylon.js engine (9.30): a separate app that installs beside the
main game.

- **The start screen** has the same film, music and logo as the main game, and one button: Enter the lounge.
- **The Business lounge**, rebuilt in Babylon.js to the same plan:
  - the copper-wave ceiling with warm hidden light
  - the herringbone oak floor
  - the windows onto the apron, with the sky as it is at this hour
  - the black and brass L-shaped bar, with a real mirror behind the bottles
  - the art deco fireplace with a real fire (particles) and its flickering light
  - armchairs on the carpet, sofas, the refreshment corner, four paintings and the flight boards
  - The doors stay shut: this edition is the lounge only.
  - You arrive where you do in the main game: on the welcome mat inside the doors.
- **Walking**:
  - touchscreen: a stick under your left thumb, drag on the right to look
  - keyboard and mouse
  - PlayStation DualShock 4 and DualSense (and other controllers): left stick walk, right stick look, L3 run,
    Cross use, Circle back, Options settings
- **The gear** is always on the screen, with two tabs:
  - Graphics: quality presets, resolution, frame-rate limit, anti-aliasing, shadows, the mirror, reflections on
    metal, glow, bloom, ambient occlusion, the fire, tone mapping, brightness, sharpening, field of view, and a
    frame-rate meter
  - Controls: controller look speed, dead zone, invert, swap sticks, vibration (with a test), touch look speed and
    stick size, mouse look speed and invert
- PC: a folder app. Run AirlineManagerProBabylon.exe; F11 switches full screen.
