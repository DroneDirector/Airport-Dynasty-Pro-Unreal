# 0.3.37

## People who move
- The cabin crew and the passengers walking in the cabin are now jointed figures that walk in step: legs and knees, arms swinging, a little rise and fall; they stop, turn and stand.
- The instructor in the Training room walks to and fro in front of the board, stopping to face the class.

## Talking in the cabin
- Tap anyone in the cabin (in a cabin view) and they say something, in a bubble over them, with a button to hear it.
- The crew speak for the moment: welcoming you while boarding, the trolley service in the cruise, seat belts and tray tables in the descent, goodbyes at the gate.
- Every passenger has a mood of their own (happy, fine, tired, nervous, grumpy, chatty) and answers in it.
- At each stop of the trolley the crew ask the row, and someone there answers.

## Crash sites
- In the sea: the wreck partly sunk, burning fuel and an oil slick with its rainbow sheen spreading on the water, fires low across it, and rescue boats bobbing round it with their lights flashing.
- A helicopter: the cabin on its side where it came down, the tail boom torn off a few metres away, the rotor blades broken and thrown round it, the skids bent (a damaged one stays in one piece with its rotor on).

## Your pet's bowl
- Tap the food bowl: you go down on one knee, pick it up and look at it (your pet's name on it, how full it is), then put it back down, and it is filled up.
