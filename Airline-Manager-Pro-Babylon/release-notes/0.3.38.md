# 0.3.38

## Your private suite
- A door in your office's right-hand wall (north of the bookshelves) opens on your suite: a short hall, the bathroom, the bedroom and the dressing alcove.
- The bathroom: a wall-hung toilet you can use (it flushes), a vanity with a basin to wash your hands at, a mirror that really reflects, a walk-in shower behind a glass panel (turn it on and off) and a freestanding bath you can run.
- The bedroom: a double bed with a channelled headboard you can lie down on, nightstands, a bedside lamp you switch on and off, a clock showing the time, a ceiling light on a switch by the door so the room goes completely dark, an armchair by the window with the view.
- The dressing alcove: the wardrobe opens your look.
- The doors open with a tap, a click, E or a controller.

## The bar in the lounge
- A bartender is always behind the bar: he turns to you and waves as you come near; tap him for the drinks (what each is, how strong, how much alcohol in a serving).
- He turns to the shelf, takes the bottle, sets a glass on the counter and pours; the drink rises in the glass. Then it is yours: pick it up, sip it (each sip is alcohol), put it down.
- The drink moves in your hand as you move; turn hard and some spills.
- Drunk: your walk wobbles and drifts, your knees give a little now and then, the view sways, blurs and doubles. It wears off in about 10 to 25 seconds, the more you drank the longer.
