# 0.3.89

## 3D from the map, for your own aircraft too
- Tap one of your own aircraft on the map, in the air or taxiing, and its pop-up now has a **3D** button ("Follow in 3D") that takes you straight into the 3D view of it. Other airlines' aircraft already had it.

## Left and right side views
- The 3D view now has both a **Left side** and a **Right side** camera, next to Behind, Front, Above, Below and the others.

## Android
- Your airport's walk-in mode stays PC only (see 0.3.88).
