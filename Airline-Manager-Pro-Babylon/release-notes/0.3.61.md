# 0.3.61

## Smoother airport (phone and PC)
- **Jetway interiors only from close by:** the carpet, wall linings, ceiling slats, handrails, console, signs, adverts and lights inside each jetway are drawn only within about 35 m of it. From further away they can't be seen through the closed tunnel, so hundreds fewer draw calls.
- **Automatic resolution** (Settings → Graphics; on by default on phones and tablets): when the graphics chip can't keep up, the picture is drawn a little smaller (never below 60%), and sharp again as soon as it can. It leaves the resolution alone when the processor is what struggles, since a softer picture wouldn't help.
- **Rain and snow on the graphics chip:** the drops and flakes are worked out by the graphics chip instead of the processor. They look the same.
- **Map tiles kept on the device:** the ground's heights and the satellite pictures are kept after the first download, so the airport and the 3D views open faster the next time, also without a connection.
