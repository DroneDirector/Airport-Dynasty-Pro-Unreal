# Airport Dynasty Pro 0.3.11

**The courtroom, on Babylon.js.**

- **The hall**, to the main game's measures:
  - panelled walnut walls under plaster, and a coffered ceiling with gilt rosettes;
  - five tall windows with red velvet curtains and the daylight falling through them;
  - four brass chandeliers;
  - the judges' bench on its dais under the emblem of justice, with high-backed chairs and the gavel;
  - the clerk's desk, the witness box, the tables of the prosecution and the defence, and the families' counsel;
  - the bar with its turned balusters and gate;
  - seven rows of public benches, paintings, the court's two screens, the doors with the exit sign, and a clock
    showing the real time.
- **A hearing:** the game runs it as before. Every line, your answers, your lawyer's suggestions and objections, the
  exhibits and the verdict are in its dialogue box, now drawn over the Babylon.js hall.
  - The camera moves to whoever is speaking. Drag to look round.
  - The exhibits appear on the court's screens.
- **The people** (stand-in figures until the full characters come):
  - the judges in black with white bands, counsel in dark suits, and the police in navy with caps;
  - the relatives in black, you in a suit with a tie in your airline's colour, and the public in everyday clothes.

  They sit at their places, stand to speak (counsel and you), turn to look at whoever is speaking, and nod and
  gesture as they talk. The witnesses take the stand in turn.
- **The empty courtroom** (no hearing today):
  - Walk about it, and jump to the bench, the witness box, the defence table, the public benches or the doors.
  - Push the gate in the bar open, and leave through the doors.
