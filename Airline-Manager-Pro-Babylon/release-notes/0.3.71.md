# 0.3.71

## Airport mode: six quick views
- A Views row under Jump, Crouch, Prone and Sprint, with a button for each place:
  1. On the apron by gate 4's jetway (where airport mode starts)
  2. On the apron, looking along the terminal to the jetways
  3. At the kerb, looking at the main entrance
  4. At the kerb, with the shuttle bus and the row of trees
  5. Inside gate 4's jetway corridor, looking out to the aircraft
  6. In gate 4, looking out of the window at the apron
- Each view puts you on a clear spot: never inside a wall, seat, rail, floor or roof. If something is in the way, you are moved to the nearest free spot.

## Walking through the jetways
- The doors between the gates and their jetway corridors no longer stop you. In airport mode the doors had been fixed in place along with the building, so they never slid open, and their glass blocked the way. All doors now open as you come to them, and they never block you.
- You can walk from the gate, through the corridor and the rotunda, and down the tunnel, and back again.

## Graphics: Scan this device
- Settings → Graphics → Scan this device. It checks the processor (its cores and a short speed test), the memory, the graphics chip and what it supports, the screen and, in a 3D view, how fast it is running now.
- It recommends a quality and the settings to go with it, shows what would change, and lets you Apply or Keep my settings.
- The result fits every screen: a sheet from the bottom on phones, a card in the middle on tablets, and two columns on a PC (facts on the left, recommendations on the right).
