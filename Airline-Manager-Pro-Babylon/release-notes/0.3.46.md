# 0.3.46

## Twelve new graphics switches
Each is its own switch in Settings → Graphics, so every one can be turned on or off on phones, tablets and the PC. The presets set them for you: the heavy ones start on only where the device can carry them. A change applies the next time the airport is opened.

- **Sky shadows:** light and shade from the whole sky, not just the sun (very heavy; on at Ultra on the PC).
- **Many real lights:** every light panel in the jetways lights what is round it (High on the PC, Ultra everywhere).
- **Light rays:** sun rays past the buildings and the jetways (High and Ultra).
- **Height fog:** a low haze over the ground that thins with height and thickens in rain (Medium and up).
- **Live reflections:** floors, glass and the wet apron reflect what is round them (very heavy; on at Ultra on the PC).
- **Soft contact shadows:** shadows are sharp where things touch the ground and softer further away (High and Ultra).
- **Temporal anti-aliasing:** no shimmer on handrails, fences and trusses (High and Ultra).
- **Eye adaptation:** brighter when you walk indoors, back down outside, eased like an eye (on everywhere).
- **Lens flares:** flares when looking towards the sun (Medium and up).
- **Depth of field:** focus on what you look at, with the rest softly blurred (off by default).
- **Motion blur:** blur when turning quickly (off by default).
- **Surface depth:** relief on the floors, the cladding and the tyres (High and Ultra).
- **Wet ground in rain:** the ground darkens and shines while it rains, then dries slowly (on everywhere).
