# 0.3.84

## Jetways: their wheels, steps, posts and rails drawn together
- Each jetway's wheels (tyres, treads, rims and hub caps), stair wheels, stair steps, posts and rails, about 390 pieces across the four jetways, are now drawn as one list per kind of piece per jetway: 60 lists instead of 390 separate objects.
- Every piece still moves on its own. The wheels steer and roll, the stair's wheels roll, and the steps tip to stay level as the stair tilts. The lists follow them only while a jetway docks or undocks. While it is parked, they cost nothing.
- You can still walk up the jetway's stair, and the pieces still cast their shadows.
