# 0.3.15 – The terminal, cleaned up

- No more flickering surfaces: ceilings, floors, roof slabs, mats and rugs no longer sit in the same plane as each other, and the depth precision is much finer, so far walls and roofs stay steady.
- One wall between two rooms instead of two side by side, so door frames no longer poke through a second wall and the doorways are clean.
- The entrances have one pane of glass instead of two, and the glass walls are drawn in the right order.
- You keep a little more distance from walls, door frames and stair sides, so the view no longer slips into them.
- Furniture that stood in the middle of the Grand Hall, the airside lobby, the landing, the link and the walkway now stands along the walls, about 10 cm clear of them; anything that reached into a wall has been moved back into its room.
- The gates, the view bays, the food walk, the bars, the lounge, the Academy and the offices keep their layouts as designed.
