# 0.3.18 – Sky, weather, drone and photos in the Terminal demo

- The terminal now has the 3D viewer's sky over it: its blue and its sunsets, the real sun, moon and stars for the time and the place, and high volumetric clouds lit by the sun.
- Weather, under the new Weather button on the right: clear, scattered, broken, overcast, storm or thunderstorm. Thunderstorms bring lightning: a bolt, the flash lighting up the clouds and the ground, and thunder that comes later the further away it struck.
- Rain or snow falls around you outside, but not indoors. It's heavier in a storm, and haze closes in.
- Time of day on a slider, with a time-lapse: paused, 1×, 60×, 600×, 2000× or 3200×. The sun, the shadows and the light in and around the terminal follow it.
- Drone: a free camera you can fly anywhere, through walls and over the roofs. Use W A S D or the stick, Space / R to go up, C / F to go down, and Shift to go faster. On a touch screen, use the ▲ ▼ buttons. Press V to switch between drone and walking.
- Photo: press the camera button or P. Everything on the screen is hidden, the picture is taken and saved, and then the interface comes back. Photos go into Pictures / Airport Dynasty Pro on Android and on the PC.
- Keys: T opens the weather panel.
