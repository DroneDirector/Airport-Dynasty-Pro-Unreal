# 0.3.83

## Instancing and texture arrays in every place
- The lounge, your office, Operations, the hangar, the court, the joined building and the terminal now get the same treatment as the airport. Shortly after a place opens, the game watches which objects stay still. Those that are not animated and are not doors, lifts, screens, people, animals, vehicles or aircraft are combined: texture arrays, merging, thin instances (with boxes to walk against) and the octree.
- Anything added later, furniture or pictures, is included on its own the next time the place opens.

## Your own models, shared
- When the same model of yours fills several slots, the copies are now instances of one model, drawn together, instead of full copies each. Models with bones or shape animation are still copied in full, as those can't be shared.

## Parked cars
- The 147 parked cars and the rental cars are now thin instances too, with boxes to walk against. A car that needs to drive off is first taken back out, its parts back where they were and its box gone. Nothing drives yet.

## Problem report at the start screen
- If any of this can't be done (instancing, texture arrays, merging, the octree), the part is simply drawn as before, and the problem is noted.
- The next time the start screen opens, a report lists what went wrong, where and why, like a crash screen, with Copy, Share and OK.
- The problems also go into the game's log. On the PC they are written to Documents\Airport Dynasty Pro\Logs\problems.log.
