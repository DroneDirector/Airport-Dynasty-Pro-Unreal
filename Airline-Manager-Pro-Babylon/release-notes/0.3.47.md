# 0.3.47

## Rain on the ground
- **Wet ground:** when it rains, asphalt and concrete slowly darken and take on a sheen; the markings get wet too. Grass and the landscape round the airport get only a little darker, and don't shine.
- **Puddles:** they collect in the hollows of the apron, the taxiways and the roads. They spread the longer it rains and dry from the edges once it stops, with a darker damp ring round each one. They are smooth as a mirror and reflect the sky, the terminal and the lights at night (most clearly with Live reflections on).
- **Ripples:** while it rains, the drops ring out across the puddles.
- All of it follows the **Wet ground in rain** switch in Settings → Graphics.

## Jetway wheels
- The wheels are closed behind the rim now: the inner half of the rim, the rim's inside and a brake back plate. You can no longer see through them past the hub, from either side.
