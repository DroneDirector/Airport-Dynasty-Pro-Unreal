# Airport Dynasty Pro 0.3.6

**The Operations office, on Babylon.js.** In the game, open **Operations** in the menu.

- **You sit at the long oak desk** in front of an ultra-wide curved monitor with your schedule as it is now:
  - the same timeline as the Schedule screen, with the next departures, delays and cancellations;
  - drawn by the game and kept up to date every few seconds.
- **The monitor**, or the buttons below it, open **Operations, the Schedule, Dispatch or the Flight planner** in the
  game.
- **Around you:**
  - archive folders stacked on the desk, and a tall archive rack full of binders and boxes;
  - a red digital clock showing the time;
  - a window onto a control tower and an apron at dusk;
  - a strip in your company colour along the wall.
- **The chair** stands you up so you can walk about, and sits you down again. **The door** goes back to the game.
- Every graphics setting applies here.

**The hangars, on Babylon.js.** In the game, open **Hangars** on the Overview and **Walk in**.

- **A hangar as big as the aircraft in it needs**, its doors shut:
  - steel columns and roof trusses, insulated wall panels, skylights across the roof and a band of windows high up,
    lit by the time of day;
  - rows of high-bay lamps, and the sliding door leaves across the whole front.
- **Your aircraft in for work**, in your colours and standing on its gear, with work stands under the engines and by
  the nose, tool trolleys, cones and the yellow safety lines.
- **The panel** shows the aircraft, its base, what is being fixed, how far the work is (%) and which mechanics are on
  it.
- **Along the back wall:** the maintenance office, which opens Maintenance in the game, and the break room with its
  vending machine.
- **The doors** go back to the game.

**Coming later:** the mechanics working round the aircraft and talking to you, and using the vending machine, with
the character animations.
