# 0.3.17 – The rental car centre

- A rental car centre now stands beside the terminal's east wing: design 1, Wing. One thin roof sweeps over the customer hall like a wing, white on top and warm timber underneath, its leading edge reaching out over the pick-up lanes on four raked white columns.
- The glass customer hall has terrazzo floors and a slatted timber ceiling, with desks for six rental companies: Nordrive, Aurora Rent, Fjord Motors, Velo Cars, Skyline and Greenline EV. Each desk has its own sign and colours, with queue lines and seating in front.
- Behind the hall is the ready/return garage: three decks and its roof, 140 bays on each level, wrapped in perforated aluminium. It has a spiral ramp at its east end, and the quick turnaround (wash and charge) is next to the ramp.
- A covered glass walk runs from the terminal's east wing to the hall. You can walk the pick-up lanes, the hall, the covered walk and the garage's ready/return level.
- Rental cars wait in the pick-up lanes: sedans, station cars, jeeps and sports cars, electric, petrol, hybrid and diesel, each with its company, number plate and bay. Two shuttle buses stand at their stop.
- Every tree, car, desk and sign is its own object with its own id and name. The cars carry everything they need to become rentals you can drive away later. Their bodies are stand-ins until the real models arrive.
- Textures throughout: timber, perforated aluminium, board-marked concrete, decks with painted bay lines, paving, asphalt, terrazzo, birch bark and leaves.
