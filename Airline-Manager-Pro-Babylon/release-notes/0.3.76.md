# 0.3.76

## Inside the terminal and in the jetways: no more slowdown from eye adaptation
- Eye adaptation (brighter indoors, back down outside) changed the picture's brightness a little every frame. Each change made every material in the airport re-check itself, every frame. Indoors and in the jetways, where the light keeps changing, that took hundreds of milliseconds a frame on phones and tablets (the "effects" part of the CPU meter).
- The brightness is now set straight on the final picture, and only when it changes noticeably. The materials are no longer disturbed. Eye adaptation looks and works the same.
