# 0.3.77

## Airport mode: named places to go to
- The quick views now have names instead of numbers, in a "Go to" grid under Jump, Crouch, Prone and Sprint:
  - Entrance: outside the main entrance doors
  - Main hall: inside the main hall, facing check-in
  - Gate 4: in the gate, looking out at the apron
  - Jetway: inside gate 4's jetway corridor
  - Apron: by gate 4's jetway (where airport mode starts)
  - Pier: on the apron, looking along the terminal
  - Kerb: at the kerb, looking at the main entrance
  - Bus stop: at the kerb, with the shuttle bus and the trees
- Two of them are new: Entrance and Main hall. Each place still puts you on a clear spot, never inside anything.
- The names are sized to fit phones, tablets and PC screens.
