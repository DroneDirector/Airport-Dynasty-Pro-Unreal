## Scan this device: realistic recommendations
- The scan now goes by how fast the game really ran on your device. While you play, the game keeps the average frame rate from your last few minutes in the 3D places, with the quality and resolution it ran at. Pauses and menus are left out.
- If it ran below 20 frames a second, the recommendation is a step lighter than what you ran. Below 35, it is never heavier. The scan says what it measured.
- Phones and tablets are recommended Medium at most, and Low on weaker chips or when the measured frame rate is low. Their resolution is set to draw about 1.3 million pixels at most (a sharp phone screen doesn't need more), 50% when it runs slowly. MSAA is never recommended on them.
- Before you have played a little, the scan says "Measured: not yet" and goes by the hardware alone.
