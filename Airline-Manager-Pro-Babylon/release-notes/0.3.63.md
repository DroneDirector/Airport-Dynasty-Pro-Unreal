# 0.3.63

## Fewer hiccups (phone and PC)
- **Jetway hoses:** the hoses' cloth movement is now worked out without making new objects every frame. The tubes are reshaped only when a hose has really moved, at most 30 times a second, and not at all when you are more than 90 m from that jetway, where the sway can't be seen.
- **The airport map** draws without making new objects for every point.
- **The effects** (the sun, the light rays, the glint) no longer make new objects every frame either.
- Fewer new objects means fewer of the short pauses when the device clears out its memory.
