# Airport Dynasty Pro 0.3.0

**The whole game interface**, opened from the title screen.

- **Continue game** goes back to your airline, at the Overview.
- **New game**: create your pilot and choose the difficulty, then found your airline.
- **Load game**: pick a saved game, the autosave or an automatic backup, or open a backup file.
- **Save game**: save your airline in a slot (overwrite or delete slots, delete all saves).
- **UI**: straight into the game's menus and screens. It stays on the title screen so you can always jump in and see
  what has changed.

**Every screen and menu is there**: the Overview with the world map, fleet and market, routes, jobs, schedule,
dispatch, planner, maintenance, finance, logbook, pilots and crew, ranking, career, news, co-op and the game's own
settings. Search, notifications, the company switcher, the balance and the clock are in the top bar.

**What's coming**

- The 3D places (your office, the Operations office, the hangars, the lounges, the courtroom, the museum and the
  rest) are being rebuilt on Babylon.js. Until each is ready, opening it says so.
- The **3D view** of a flight opens the 3D viewer.

**Getting round**

- The logo at the top of the menu goes back to the title screen (it saves first).
- The power button quits the game.
- The title screen now lists the game first, with the demos (3D viewer, Lounge) below.
