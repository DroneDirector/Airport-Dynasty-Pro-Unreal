## Cabin views in the 3D viewer
- The cabin, built inside the aircraft: sidewalls with the windows where they are on the outside (the wing, the engine, the clouds and the ground outside), the ceiling light strip, overhead bins with mood lighting, carpet with a lighter aisle, and galley bulkheads at both ends.
- 27 rows of slimline seats, three and three, in the airline's colours, with extra legroom at the overwing exits.
- Passengers in their seats, as many as the flight carries.
- Boarding: passengers come in at the front door and walk to their row; deboarding: they stand up and leave.
- In the cruise a cabin crew member brings the trolley down the aisle, stopping at each row; another walks the cabin now and then. The lights dim at night.
- New views: Left window and Right window (over the wing), Aisle seat, and Walk the cabin (move along the aisle and look round).
