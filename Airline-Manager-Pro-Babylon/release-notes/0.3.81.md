# 0.3.81

## Reflections: Off, Live or Screen (Settings → Graphics)
- A new Reflections choice, on every quality and on phones and the PC. Only one can be on at a time. It is Off unless you choose one, and it stays as you set it.
- Live: the terminal's outer glass mirrors what is really there, the jetways, the parked aircraft and the sky, even when they are behind you.
  - The glass on the side of the terminal you are on is mirrored: on the apron side, every gate window on both floors and the glass between; on the kerb side, the entrance and the Grand Hall's front. Walk round and it switches.
  - The jetways' windows get their own small all-round live reflection while you are on the apron side.
  - Nothing is mirrored while you are inside, where you see out through the glass.
  - Its slider sets the quality: from a small mirror drawn every 3rd frame with just the jetways, aircraft and sky, to a sharp one every frame with the airfield and the ground too.
- Screen: the floors and the wet apron reflect what is on screen (before, this was "Live reflections", on Ultra on the PC only). Its slider sets how sharp, how far and how strong. It applies when the airport is reopened.
