# 0.4.2

## Flights keep clear of the mountains
- In the 3D view, a flight now looks 8 km ahead at the ground and keeps 300 m above it, climbing or easing down smoothly instead of flying into a slope or sliding along it. Where the ground ahead is far above its planned height, it eases a little to the side where the ground is lower, and back once clear. On final, within 6 km of the airport, it follows its approach to the runway. Everywhere, Vágar included.
