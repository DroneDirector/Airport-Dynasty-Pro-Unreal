# 0.3.28 – Sprint at full speed

- Airport mode: sprinting now gives its full speed, about 6 m/s, four times walking pace. The step along the floor and the fall were moved together, so the floor underneath ate most of each step. They are now moved separately, so walking, sprinting and crawling all reach the speeds they should.
- Sprint now works in any direction you move, not only straight ahead.
