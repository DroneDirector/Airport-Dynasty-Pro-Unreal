# 0.3.90

## Your airport in 3D, from the map
- A new button with a small terminal-and-tower icon sits right under the **3D** button on the Overview map and in the Map section. Tap it to go straight to your own airport in the 3D view: its runway, taxiways, stands and the terminal's outline, seen from above. Drag to look around and zoom in or out.
- It's on Android and the PC. The walk-in airport mode stays on the PC only.
