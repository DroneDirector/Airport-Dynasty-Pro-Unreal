# 0.4.0

## Walking in the world: fixes
- **No more ending up inside a mountainside.** Your feet now follow the ground exactly as it is drawn, so on steep slopes you stand on the slope you see, never under it. When you're placed in the world you drop the last bit onto the ground, and you fall down a step rather than floating over it.
- **The holographic map** sits in the bottom-left corner again, clear of Exit and the gear.
- **Record video** only shows while Cinema is open, together with a new **✕ Exit cinema** button in the top-left corner (Esc still works too).
- **Double-tap anywhere** on the screen now brings the controls back after Hide UI, on touch screens too.

## Also in this version (since 0.3.92)
- Walk anywhere in the world from the map, with Jump, Crouch, Prone and Run, Cinema (photos and videos), Hide UI and Back to the overview.
- The holographic map's one-tap destinations, floor arrows and speed zoom.
- Your airport button on the map (PC).
