# 0.3.25 – Walk your own airport

- Airport, in the game's left menu, now takes you to your own airport at its site. You arrive on foot on the kerb just outside the main entrance.
- In airport mode you walk with gravity: on the floors, up the stairs, over the kerb and the apron. You can't go through walls, glass, furniture or the aircraft, and the doors open as you come to them. Walking like this is only in airport mode, the one from the game's menu.
- The 3D viewer's Airport button now brings you in above the car park, looking at the Grand Hall under its wave roof. From there you have the free drone camera and the views.
- The Gate 4 view starts behind the front row of seats in gate 4, with the gate desk and its sign to your right, looking straight out at the aircraft, the apron and the mountains.
