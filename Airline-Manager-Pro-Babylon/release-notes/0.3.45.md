# 0.3.45

## A new look for the airport
- The sky lights everything: its colour and light (the sunset, the night) are reflected in the jetways' steel and cladding, the glass and the aircraft, and follow the time of day.
- Ambient occlusion (Ultra): soft shadow in corners, under the jetways, round the wheels and along the walls.
- Sharper, longer shadows across the apron (Ultra on the PC).
- Filmic colour, richer bloom on the lights, a light vignette and sharpening (High and Ultra), with a fine film grain on the PC at Ultra.
- Choose the level in Settings → Graphics: Ultra shows all of it. Phones keep a lighter version so they stay smooth.
