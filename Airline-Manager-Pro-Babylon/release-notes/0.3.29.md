# 0.3.29 – Photo mode in the 3D viewer

- The 3D viewer has a Photo mode button at the top left. The controls go away and a strip at the bottom offers:
  - Looks: Natural, Warm, Cinematic, Black and white, Vivid and Vintage, shown live on the screen.
  - Wide frame: black bars for a 2.39:1 cinema frame.
  - Depth of field (off, soft, strong): the aircraft stays sharp, and what's nearer or further away blurs by its distance. The focus follows the aircraft as you move the camera.
  - A caption, written along the bottom of the picture.
- The shutter button takes the picture with everything else off the screen. It's made at up to 4K, with the look, the wide frame, a soft vignette for Cinematic and Vintage, and the caption. It's saved to Pictures / Airport Dynasty Pro on Android and the PC.
- You can still move the camera as usual in photo mode. The ✕ takes you back to the viewer.
