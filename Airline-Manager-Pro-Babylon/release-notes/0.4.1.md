# 0.4.1

## The ground is always there under you
- Walking in the world (and in the 3D view), the ground is now drawn from every side, so it never disappears under your feet or close by, on slopes too.

## Following a flight in 3D: a clean screen
- Top left: **Back to the overview**, **Cinema** and **Hide UI** (double-tap, double-click or Esc brings the controls back).
- Bottom right, the cameras: **Cabin** (walk the cabin), **L wing**, **R wing** and **Spot** (outside: drag to circle round the aircraft).
- Bottom left, the holographic map:
  - **Outside:** the land round the aircraft as far as you can see from outside, with the coastlines as glowing lines and the land inside them in a darker holographic blue, turned so the aircraft points up.
  - **Inside:** the aircraft's plan (fuselage, seats, wings and tail) with you on it, moving as you walk the cabin.
- On Android and the PC.
