# 0.3.49

## Airport map while you walk
- A square, holographic blue map in the top-right corner when you walk in the terminal or in airport mode. It's always centred on you and turns with you, so up is the way you face.
- It shows the walls with their doorways open, the stairs, the lifts, the columns, the kerb, the apron and the rental car centre, with the rooms' names where they fit. The room you are in is lit.
- The floor you are on is drawn clearly. The other floor shows only faintly, on both floors.
- The map fills in as you walk: places appear once you get near them and stay on the map next time.
- Press M, or tap the map, to make it bigger or smaller.
