# 0.3.85

## Lighter glow and shadow passes
- Every frame the airport is drawn more than once: the main view, the shadows (seen from the sun) and the glow of lamps, signs and screens. The extra passes took more draw calls than the main view.
- Glow: things that glow are always in it. The big things that only stop glow showing through walls are now in it only within 120 m of you, where it matters. The glow pass takes about a quarter to a half fewer draw calls (at a gate, 400 down to 190).
- Shadows: small things under about 0.8 m (bins, posts, cables, small signs) cast shadows only within 40 m of you, where their shadows can be seen. Big things follow the shadow distance as before.
- In our test at gate 4, a frame went from about 2,200 draw calls to about 1,800. The look stays the same.
