# 0.3.70

## No stutter when hidden things first come into view
- Things that start out hidden or switched off (the insides of the jetways, far-away detail, lift cars, rental cars and the like) now have their graphics prepared in the background, a few at a time, right after the airport or terminal opens.
- Before, they were prepared the moment they first came into view, which could cause a short stutter. The picture is the same.
