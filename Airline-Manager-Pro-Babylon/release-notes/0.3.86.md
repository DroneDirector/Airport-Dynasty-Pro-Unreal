# 0.3.86

## Settings: nothing overlaps any more
- In Settings, a setting with a long description and several buttons (Reflection type, Shadow updates) could run over the settings next to it when the list was long. Each setting now always takes the room it needs, and its buttons drop onto their own line under its text when they don't fit beside it. On phones, tablets and the PC.
- Two settings were both called Reflections. They are now Sky reflections (metal and paint reflect the sky and the room) and Reflection type (Off, Live, Screen or Both).

## Real departures and arrivals on the terminal's screens
- The timetable screens in the main hall (arrivals on the right, departures at the other end), the totems and the small screens now show the real flights at the airport: your own and the other airlines' traffic, as the game's own boards do. They're asked for every 4 seconds, so a change shows within a few seconds.
- Every screen now looks like a real monitor: a satin bezel with a lit edge, a glowing panel, a coloured header with a plane icon, status pills in their colours, a live light with the time of the last change, and the glass's sheen.
- The totems are a little wider (and their stands with them) so every line fits.
- Sample flights appear only while there's no information at all; they go away for good at alpha.

## The operations room's video wall
- The huge screen along the wall is now six monitors side by side: departures, arrivals, departures, arrivals… each showing the next page of its kind. While there are too few flights to fill it, the pages repeat in that order; as traffic grows, new flights take the repeats' places.

## Performance overlay at the bottom
- The overlay now sits low at the bottom in the middle, clear of the other buttons, in three short rows: the names (Frames, CPU, GPU, Drawing, RAM, VRAM), their figures under them, and a line that says what holds the frame rate back when it struggles.
- Drag it anywhere with the mouse or a finger; it stays where you leave it. Double-tap to put it back.

## Go to buttons
- The view buttons are back in the bottom-right corner (they had moved to the top over the overlay) and are only as wide as their names.
