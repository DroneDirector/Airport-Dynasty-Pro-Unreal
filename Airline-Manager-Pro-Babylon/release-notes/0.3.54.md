# 0.3.54

## A free mouse pointer (PC)
- Press the mouse wheel down (the middle button) and the mouse stops turning the view: you get the mouse pointer back, to click on things. Press the wheel down again to look round with the mouse again.
- Works in the airport and in every place you walk around in.

## PlayStation 5 controller on the PC
- The DualSense (PS5) and the DualShock 4 (PS4) now work fully on the PC, over USB and over Bluetooth. On Windows they often report their buttons and sticks in their own order; the game now reads that order, so walking, looking, running, the D-pad in menus, Cross, Circle and Options all work as they do on Android. Vibration works where the PC allows it.
