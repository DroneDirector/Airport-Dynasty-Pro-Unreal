# 0.3.51

## Airport map
- **Traced from the real 3D walls:** each time the airport opens, the map is drawn from the walls, columns and glass actually built, cut through at waist height on each floor, one clean line per wall. Any change to the 3D terminal shows on the map automatically.
- **One floor at a time:** every wall belongs to its own room's floor. Tall walls, like the Grand Hall's, no longer show bright on the floor above.
- **Other floors as a ghost:** floors above are lifted slightly up the map and floors below sunk slightly down it, drawn thin, dashed and very dim, like the layers of a hologram. Works for any floors added later.
- **Names:** only the rooms on your floor are named, with no overlapping labels; the room you're in comes first.
- **Which floor:** "FLOOR 1" or "FLOOR 2" in the corner, and a small stack of floors at the side with yours lit.
