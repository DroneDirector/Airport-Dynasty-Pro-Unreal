# Airport Dynasty Pro 0.3.4

**The ground under the flights in the 3D viewer.** The airports of the flight you follow are built from the game's
own layouts (the simulator's, OpenStreetMap's, or laid out from the runways).

- **Runways** with their real markings:
  - threshold bars, designators, the centre line, touchdown zone and aiming point marks, and the edge lines;
  - from dusk, their lights: white edge lights, green thresholds and the approach lights.
- **Taxiways** with their yellow centre lines, the **aprons**, and the **stands** with their lead-in lines, stop bars
  and numbers.
- **Terminal buildings.**
- **The ground**: the airport stands at its own height, with fields, woods and towns round it out to the horizon.
- **Landing gear**: down on the ground and below about 750 m on the way in or out, up in the climb. It takes 8
  seconds, the nose leg folding forward and the main legs inwards.
- **Landing and taxi lights** shine ahead of the aircraft at dusk and at night, close to the ground.

- **Real terrain** under flights below 33,000 ft:
  - the mountains, hills, valleys, coasts, fjords and the sea where they really are, on the curve of the Earth,
    covered with satellite pictures;
  - about 45 km across round the aircraft, laid out again as it flies on;
  - loaded over the internet, and on or off in Settings → Graphics → Real terrain.
  - Terrain: Terrain Tiles on AWS. Imagery: Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 by EOX (CC BY 4.0).
