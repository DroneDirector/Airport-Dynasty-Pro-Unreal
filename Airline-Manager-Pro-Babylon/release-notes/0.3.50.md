# 0.3.50

## Snow on the ground
- When it snows, the grass and the land round the airport turn white over a few minutes.
- The runway, the taxiways and the aprons look cleared by ploughs: dark, wet tarmac with snow left in the cracks, in patches and streaks along the runway, thicker along the edges and thinner in the wheel tracks. Markings get partly covered.
- When the snow stops, it melts over about ten minutes and leaves the ground wet.

## Sun glint
- The sun glints on the tarmac, the concrete and the markings, from the sun's side, the way it does low in the evening. It's brighter on wet ground.
- It only glints when the sun actually gets through the clouds: none behind thick cloud, but sunshine through a gap in the clouds glints where it lands. The same check now also dims the sunlight and its shadows under clouds.
- It's cheap to draw, and has its own switch: Settings → Graphics → Sun glint.

## Settings
- "Wet ground in rain" is now "Rain and snow on the ground".

## Airport map
- The map now shows everything on your floor, right out to its edges and corners, not just the area round you.
