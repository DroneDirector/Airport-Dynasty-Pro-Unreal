# 0.3.62

## Controllers: flight gear stays out of the way
- **Flight gear is never used for walking or the menus:** flight sticks, throttles, rudder pedals, yokes and panels (such as the Saitek X-56) stay free for flying. The game takes a gamepad (PlayStation, Xbox or any standard one) instead.
- **No more drifting menus:** an axis that rests away from the centre, like a throttle or a slider at one end, is left out, so it can't move through the menus or walk you away by itself. This applies in the 3D places and in the game's own menus.
- **Choose your controller:** with more than one connected, Settings → Controls has "Controller to use": Automatic, or any connected controller (flight gear is marked).

## Smoother (phone and PC)
- **Route finding in the background:** the airport map's route is worked out in a background thread, so the game never waits for it.
- **Tiny things far away:** things under about a metre across (small fittings, bolts, hubs, small signs) are left out beyond about 180 m, where they would be a pixel or two. Lights and anything that glows always stay.
