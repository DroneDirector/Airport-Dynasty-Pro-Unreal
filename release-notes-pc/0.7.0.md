## Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.7.0: the interface while you walk

- **The holographic map** in the corner, as on Android: centred on you and turned the way you face, the walls with
  their doorways, the stairs, lifts and columns, the other floor as a faint ghost, your arrow and view cone.
- **The full map** (M, or a click on the small map): the whole floor, north up, with the rooms' names. Click where you
  want to go and an orange route is drawn there, through the doors and up or down the stairs; it follows you and
  clears when you arrive.
- **A clean screen**: Map, Settings and Hide UI in the top-left corner. H hides everything; a double-click or Esc
  brings it back.
- **Settings** (Tab): graphics quality from Low to Cinematic, the render scale, a frame limit, VSync, the performance
  overlay, Scan my PC, and the problem log.
- **Your PC is scanned on the first start** and the graphics set to suit it.
- **The performance overlay** (P): frames per second, and the game, render and graphics card times.
- **The problem log**: every warning and error is written to a file, so a problem can be sent and fixed.
- Everything from 0.5.0 and 0.6.0: the terminal and its passengers.
