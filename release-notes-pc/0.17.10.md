# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.17.10

- **Puddles and wet ground.** The runway, the taxiways and the aprons now get wet: the pavement darkens and turns glossy, and puddles appear at random spots (a few on the runway and taxiways, more on the aprons), with darker damp rims and the sky reflected in the water. While it rains, rain ripples run across them; when the rain stops they dry slowly from their edges in, the way they do in a cool climate like Iceland's (about three hours, adjustable).
- **Rainy day switch.** The real rain isn't simulated yet, so Settings has a **Rainy day** switch: turn it on and the ground soaks over about a minute, the puddles fill and the ripples start; turn it off and everything dries slowly. A wetness slider sets it wet or dry at once.
- **Tune them yourself** in Settings: how many puddles, their size, how mirror-like the water is, the ripples' strength, and the drying time. Your values are saved and kept.
- Also in this version: the t-shirt (crew neck, our own "Northern Skies" print) is built for your character.
