## Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.12.0: doors, announcements and frame generation

- **The doors open**: the terminal's 51 doors open as you walk up to them and shut behind you, as in the Babylon
  edition: sliding glass doors part, swing and double doors turn on their hinges, the revolving doors keep turning.
  Every area of the terminal can be reached.
- **Airport announcements from your game's real flights**: boarding, delays, the final call, the gate closing, and late
  passengers called to their gate by name. Every now and then a passenger is asked to the information desk, or there
  is a notice for everyone (unattended baggage, liquids, no smoking, a found wallet).
  - The announcer is a woman, heard through the loudspeakers with a chime first. She uses the game's natural voice
    when it is on your PC, otherwise Windows' own female voice.
  - Loud and clear in the public areas: the main hall, the long walkway, the gates upstairs and below, food and
    tax-free. Faint and muffled in offices and Operations, silent in the toilets and outside the building.
  - Only in the 3D view; every announcement is also in the Overview's messages, word for word.
  - Volume sliders in Settings (Tab): all announcements, flight calls, passenger calls, information.
- **Frame generation (AMD FSR)**: a switch in Settings → Graphics, for any graphics card; off unless you turn it on.
- **A frame-rate box** (Settings) that you can drag anywhere: press the **middle mouse button** to free the pointer,
  drag the box, press it again to look round.
- **First start**: the game measures your PC and suggests a graphics quality; nothing changes unless you say yes.
