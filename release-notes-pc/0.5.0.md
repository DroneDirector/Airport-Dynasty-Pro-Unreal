## Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.5.0: the terminal you can walk around in

The first PC release on Unreal Engine 5. It installs beside the current PC app.

- **The whole passenger terminal**, both floors, built from the same layout as the Android edition, in its materials:
  terrazzo halls, carpeted gates, tiled toilets, the lounge's herringbone, white cladding.
- **Glass walls** with clear panes between slim white frames.
- **71 doors** that open as you walk up and close behind you: sliding glass, revolving, swing, secure staff doors with
  card readers, the one-way door out of security and the jetway doors, with each room's name over its door.
- **Every room furnished**: the 24 check-in desks with belts and queue lines, bag drops, trees, the six security
  lanes, the lounge with its copper wave ceiling, fireplace and bar, offices, Operations, the Academy, the crew room,
  the baggage hall, the Food Walk with its eateries, Tax Free, the family room, the oval bar, the moving walkways,
  seating at all eight gates, and the ceiling lights.
- **Stairs** with oak and walnut steps and glass balustrades, and **five working lifts** that come when you walk up
  and ride you gently to the other floor.
- **The four jetways** from the upper gates out to their stands, walkable to the cab.
- **Timetable screens** in all 23 places (sample flights until the game's world comes to the PC version).
