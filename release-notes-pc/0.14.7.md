# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.14.7

- A loading screen into the airport (from the Overview's Terminal or Airport site, or the quick shortcut): it waits until everything is in (shaders, the terrain, textures, the satellite pictures), with a progress bar and a line saying what it's doing. Esc goes straight back to the menus.
- Updates show a progress bar with the percentage in its middle (light over the empty part, dark where the bar has filled it) and the download speed; the world's terrain download too.
- The title screen and the menus run smoothly: the 3D world isn't drawn behind them any more.
- At night outside, the terminal no longer washes out white (the camera brightens the dark less); the light masts a little stronger so the apron's ground stays readable.
- The entrance canopies' ceilings aren't lamps any more: matte, like the departures sign.
- The entrance doors' glass matches the big windows on the terminal's back.
- The ATC tower: downlights all round the balcony under the roof's edge (aimed away from the cab's windows) and six over the entrance, on from sunset.
