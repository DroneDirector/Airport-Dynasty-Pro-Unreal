# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.20.2

- **Aircraft to the hangar:** an aircraft can be towed from any gate or remote stand into the maintenance hangar and back again. The tow follows the taxiway lines, keeps 3 m wingtip clearance, and waits at the runway hold until the runway is free.
- **Hangar tug:** a copy of the pushback tug, used only for hangar tows. It parks by the electric bus chargers.
- **Brake rider:** rides in the cockpit during every hangar tow. He boards by the jetway service stair or a passenger stair, and the aircraft lights come on when he is aboard.
- **Hangar stair:** each hangar hall has its own passenger stair, so the brake rider can climb down inside.
- **Chocks and power:** the chocks come away and the ground power is unplugged before the tow. Wing walkers walk beside it.
- **Tow it yourself:** you can drive the hangar tow with the tug yourself.
- **Maintenance vans:** 3 vans in every new game, copied from the cleaning van. The crew ride them across the runway to the hangar.
- **Vehicles owned:** a new Vehicles page on the laptop, tablet and phone. It lists every airport vehicle you own, how many of each kind, and each one's condition, what it is doing and where it is.
- **Buy vehicles:** a Buy button for every kind of vehicle. The new one parks beside the others of its kind, and the crew start using it.

Known: these are new and not yet run in the game. Still to come for the hangar: the tower's radio call for the crossing, mechanics working where the fault is, Settings > Realism for maintenance time, the maintenance page on every device, and saving during a tow.
