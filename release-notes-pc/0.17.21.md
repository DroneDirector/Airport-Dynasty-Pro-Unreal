# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.17.21

- **16 gates.** The gate walkway now runs much further both ways. Four new gates in the west, like the ones you know, and four big gates in the east: one for the A380 with three jetway openings, three for the B777 with two each. Every new stand has its own ground markings for its aircraft types.
- **Tax free.** A big walk-through shop on the upper floor at the west end: beauty, liquor with a wine cellar and a tasting bar, the Aurora Plaza with a champagne bar under glowing aurora ribbons, an Iceland market, luxury shops and a pay lounge. A glowing neon sign marks the way in and the way out.
- **Baggage claim.** A new arrivals wing with five baggage belts, customs and the arrivals hall. The bags come from the crew side and ride round the belts.
- **The wave roof** now covers the whole terminal, from end to end.
- **Moved to make room:** the control tower stands further west, and the car rental with its garage has a new place next to the main car park.
- **New quick spots on the full map:** the tax free, the A380 gate and the baggage claim.
- **Effects settings.** In Settings > Graphics > Effects you can switch each effect on or off and choose its quality: lamp flares, ground marks, heat haze behind parked aircraft, taxiway lights, the moving LED adverts and the weather on the ground. Texture memory and shadow detail have their own sliders too.
- **New details:** blue and green taxiway lights, oil stains and tyre marks on the ground, heat haze behind parked aircraft, moving LED adverts, wet ground, puddles and snow that follow the weather, and a quality choice for the airport ground (up to 4K).
- **Jetways** line up with the front door of each aircraft type.
- **Much smoother at night.** The lamp flares made the game slow at night. They're now off unless you switch them on, and when they're on only the nearby ones are drawn.
- **Much smoother on Cinematic.** Cinematic now looks the same but runs about twice as fast.
