# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.17.12

- **Your own character in the fire truck.** Your MetaHuman, in your own clothes, now gets in. It opens the door, puts a foot on the small step, grabs the handle and pulls itself up into the middle seat, and drives with its hands on the wheel. The old stand-in body is gone. Any character works the same way, women and men alike, at their own size.
- **Getting in and out is easier.** E (X on the pad) finds the truck whenever you stand next to it and face it, in first person or seen from behind, from anywhere along the truck. You walk to the door yourself. The same button gets you out.
- **Three views while driving.** V (D-pad up) switches between the cab, close behind, and a new view much further back. The view you walked with is the view you drive with: first person gets you the cab, third person gets you the outside view. When you get out, it carries back the same way.
- **Natural walking and running.** Your body stays right under you. Before, it ran ahead of you and jumped back. Your steps match how fast you move. Running into a turn, the body turns and leans into it. The camera behind you no longer jumps while you run.
- **First person.** Looking down you see your arms, hands, legs and feet, but no longer into your own body. Your shadow is whole, head included. You can look down 80 degrees at most.
- **Drone mode.** Open the full-screen map (M) and press DRONE MODE to fly a free camera:
  - W A S D fly level, Space goes up and Ctrl goes down, and the mouse turns and tilts the view.
  - The mouse wheel sets the speed, from 0.5 to 60 m/s. Hold Shift to boost up to 300 m/s, which reaches the clouds in seconds.
  - On a pad: the sticks fly and look, the triggers go up and down, the D-pad sets the speed, L3 boosts and B leaves.
  - H hides the interface and Esc brings it back. Esc again puts you back on your feet, exactly where you stood.
- **The map's shortcut buttons** now sit beside the full-screen map, so they no longer cover any of it.
- **A smooth title screen.** The film behind the title menu is now drawn by Unreal on the graphics card, so it no longer stutters. It has the same pictures, timing, effects and colours. The neon logo, the menu and the music are unchanged.
- Install with the Setup file. Existing installs update themselves, or you can use an Update zip. There is no separate full zip, because it is over GitHub's 2 GB file limit.
