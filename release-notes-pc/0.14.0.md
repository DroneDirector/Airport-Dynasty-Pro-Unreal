# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.14.0

## The whole world's terrain
- Mountains and valleys all over the Earth: Copernicus GLO-90 worldwide, with the Faroe Islands' and Iceland's own 10 m height models on top, your airport's ground levelled as before.
- The sea everywhere it is sea: real water on every coast of the world, drawn from the terrain itself (the detailed sea round your airport, with its waves and foam, stays).
- Downloaded once by the game (about 5.4 GB in 129 region packs): Settings > The world's terrain > DOWNLOAD / UPDATE. Each pack's checksum is checked; an update later downloads only the packs that changed. Nothing streams while you play.
- Kept in your user folder by default (no Windows permission needed); MOVE IT TO ANOTHER FOLDER puts it on another drive if you like.
- Used from the next start after the download finishes. Without it the Earth stays smooth, as before.
