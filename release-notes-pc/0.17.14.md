# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.17.14

- **The airport's roads, built from your blueprint.** Everything in front of the terminal is now a real road network:
  - **The through road:** two lanes from the 3 km mark along the whole terminal front to the roundabout at the west end, with a double yellow centre line and white edge lines.
  - **The terminal forecourt:** on the terminal side, a grass island with lamps, then a spare lane, a taxi lane, taxi stands, a bus lane and bus stands, all one-way toward the roundabout. They widen in gradually where the forecourt starts and narrow back out the same way at its ends.
  - **Bus and taxi stands:** five yellow bus boxes by the main entrance, white taxi boxes toward the east end.
  - **A wide pavement in front of the terminal:** the entrance, its canopy columns, the benches and the rental area all stand on it.
  - **Zebra crossings** at all three entrances: from the terminal's pavement to the island, and on across the through road to the car park.
  - **The roundabout:** one lane wide enough for a bus, a low concrete apron for long vehicles, a raised grass middle, give-way markings on the way in. Straight on past it is closed for now; the road back to town leaves to the south.
  - **The road back to town:** two lanes, round the loop and east past the car park.
  - **The large car park** across the through road, with almost 4,000 marked spaces.
  - **The rental area:** the rental cars in marked bays, the two shuttle buses in yellow bays.
- **Street lights:** Faroese-style swan-neck lamps (galvanised poles, LED heads) along the forecourt, on the island, by the car park, round the roundabout, and on one side of the approach roads. They come on at sunset and in fog, and light the roads at night.
- **Road details:**
  - Cast-iron drain gratings in the gutter by every kerb, every 22 m.
  - Round manhole covers on the pavements, far apart.
  - A real road surface, darker than the taxiways, with polished wheel paths, an oil strip down each lane, grit by the edges and the odd repair patch. Puddles gather in the wheel ruts first, and snow wears off where the wheels run.
- **The terminal model's old landside** (its own road, car park, parked cars and roadside trees) has given way to the new roads.
