# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.17.24

- **Your tablet.** Press **Tab** (gamepad: **D-pad Down**) and you take your tablet up into your hands; press it again to put it away. Press **E** while holding it and it comes up close with its screen open, just like the phone. The mouse moves the pointer on it exactly, as on the phone and the computers. The room behind softly blurs while you hold the phone or the tablet, like a portrait photo.
- **The tablet itself:** black, slightly glossy, a small passenger aeroplane logo on the back, and the owner's own drone photo as its wallpaper.
- **Wallpapers and settings.** On the tablet, the phone and the computers you can choose a wallpaper - or your own picture, cropped the way you like - plus brightness, auto-brightness, notifications, Do not disturb and click sounds.
- **The Overview now opens only from your tablet or a computer** (its Overview app). The Overview's logo takes you back to the tablet or computer you came from, not to the title screen. G / the pad's Y and Esc no longer open it from the 3D world; Esc with nothing open now asks whether to leave to the title screen (save and leave, leave, or stay).
- **Settings moved to F10** (Tab is now the tablet).
- **New Game** starts you outside, 35 m in front of the main hall, looking straight at the centre entrance doors. **Continue / Load** put you back exactly where you were, facing the same way.
- **Jetway control screen (HMI).** The jetway cab's console has a new flat monitor running a live control screen: status strip, Home with the T-10 checklist, Docking (plan view, manual jog pad, AUTO DOCK / UNDOCK), CCTV, Status, Alarms with history, Maintenance with a system check that really runs, and Settings. From the cab it glows softly at about 10 %; press E and the camera moves up to it at full brightness. A small "+" pointer (mouse or stick) shows where you aim; E, left mouse or the pad's X clicks. Manual mode moves the jetway forward/back, left/right, up/down and turns the cab. The jetway never docks on its own.
- **Frame generation** switches on and off straight away while you play - no restart.

Still to come: hands holding the tablet, the jetway's real 3D CCTV cameras, and the crew using the jetway screen.
