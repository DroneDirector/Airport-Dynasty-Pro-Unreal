# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.19.0

- **The terminal is full again:** all 144 people are in the game (0.18.0 to 0.18.2 went out with 8 of them), with light models at a distance so a busy hall stays smooth.
- **Talking:** the camera gently goes in on the person, they stop, stay and look at you, the background blurs, and a frosted-glass card beside their face shows who they are and where they fly from and to. Works with mouse and gamepad.
- **Remote stands R0-R7:** pavement, markings and lights, passenger stairs, walking passengers, and **apron buses** you can drive and ride in - kneeling, seven doors, seats, poles, electric sounds, battery and charging.
- **The vehicle area:** every kind of ground vehicle can be bought and stands there; vehicles wear, break down, are handed over and mended. Hired crews use them.
- **Crew:** the airport hires beginners for every job by itself - cheap but slow - until you hire better people.
- **Rain on the glass:** vehicles with working wipers, jetways, towers, hangars, the fire station and the terminal's windows. Drops come and dry naturally.
- **Roads behind the terminal:** real worn surfaces and paint that hold at a distance and at night. Vehicle dirt, tyres and rims redone.
- **Boards and timetables** show the real flights only; boarding calls follow the boards; announcements like a real airport's.
- **The phone's weather** is in step with the game; the coming days come from MET Norway.
- **Performance:** graphics memory no longer overflows in the usual views (doubled textures removed, the control tower as Nanite), fewer stutters.
- Seats are known to everyone: nobody sits down on yours. The walking keys stand you up. Feet stay above the ground. Passengers come and go only where nobody sees it.

Known: the view inside the jetways is slower than elsewhere; two drivers at the forecourt stand a little below the pavement.
