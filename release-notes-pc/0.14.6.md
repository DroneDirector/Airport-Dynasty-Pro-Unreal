# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.14.6

- Inside the main hall, the signs over the three entrances read EXIT WEST, MAIN EXIT and EXIT EAST (outside they still say ENTRANCE).
- The ATC tower's lift: a light in the car; after arriving the doors stay open ten seconds (and while you stand in the doorway), so you can walk out without being caught.
- The walking view: wider (90 degrees on a 16:9 screen) and right on every screen shape, ultra-wide included (it no longer looks zoomed in); a Field of view slider in Settings (60-120 degrees).
- The holographic map stays open after a quick view (Gate 4 and the others) until you close it.
- Light beams slider in Settings (how far the light masts' beams show in the haze, 60-500 m).
- An unstuck panel: stuck against or inside something for two seconds, one click puts you on free floor nearby.
- Walk anywhere: you're put down once the ground's pictures are in too (no blurry ground on arrival).
- The crash box shows a crash only once, and only recent ones.
- The world's terrain: the Faroe Islands' and Iceland's own height models now blend smoothly into the world's (no steps or walls at their edges); Settings > DOWNLOAD / UPDATE THE WORLD'S TERRAIN fetches only the changed packs.
