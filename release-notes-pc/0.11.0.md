## Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.11.0: the business lounge

- **The business lounge, in Unreal**: the title screen's Lounge opens the Babylon edition's own lounge, imported
  exactly as it is (nothing rebuilt) with its textures: the copper wave ceiling, the herringbone oak floor, the carpet,
  the armchairs and coffee tables, the bar with its mirror wall, the fireplace, the paintings with their picture
  lights, the plants and the window wall onto the apron.
- Lit with the lounge's own lamps, the fire and the picture lights, in the same places as in the Babylon edition, by
  Unreal (Nanite, Lumen).
- G (or Y / Triangle) goes back to the menus; Terminal takes you back to where you were in the terminal.
- The airport's map stays out of the lounge.
