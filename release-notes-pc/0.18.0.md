# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.18.0

- **A second airport: Chablais Airport in Switzerland (LSCH / CLB).** At New game you choose where your airport is: Iceland or the Rhône plain between Villeneuve and Roche, with Lake Geneva and the Alps round it.
  - Real Swiss ground: swissALTI3D heights and 10-20 cm aerial photos, the real roads to the town, the lake's water with waves by the open water and the wind.
  - 1.8 million real trees, placed where the trees really are (found in swisstopo's surface model and the 10 cm photo), drawn within 4 km of you. Green all year for now; snow on the branches only when snow lies on the ground.
  - Swiss flags that really wave: a cloth simulation blown by the real wind.
  - Bilingual signs at the terminal: "Départs / Departures", "Bienvenue / Welcome to Chablais Airport".
  - The timetable boards read "Departures · Départs" on Swiss time.
  - The holographic map can show the mountains round you by name (the MOUNTAINS button).
- **The new simulation runs your airline.** Your career lives in the game's own simulation, on the real clock, and catches up on the time you were away.
  - The boards, the gate screens, the boarding calls and the phone's messages show the real flights at your airport - yours and other airlines'.
  - Crews are rostered on the real EASA flight time limits; people are promoted, ask for raises, take holidays and can be let go with notice.
  - Maintenance checks (A, C, D), ground teams for every turnaround, stands and gates, fire cover by the ICAO rules.
  - Connections, frequent flyers, alliances, share prices, going public, fuel hedging, weekly challenges and the league.
  - A night curfew and the town round the airport: win it over properly, or slip the syndic something and hope nobody finds out.
- **Aircraft in 3D (first steps).** Your aircraft stand at the stands the simulation gives them, push back, taxi on the centre lines and take off; arrivals land and taxi in.
- **The new Overview:** one live dashboard with everything at a glance.
- **Save whenever you want:** F5, or "Save now" when you press Esc, under your own name.
- **Always the real weather:** the live report near your airport sets the sky, the wind, rain puddles and snow.
- **Updates never start over:** a download carries on where it stopped, even after closing the game.
- **The world's terrain has a new version (about 37 GB, with Chablais's 10 cm photos).** Settings > The world's terrain > Download / update: only the changed packs come down, and it carries on where it stopped. Until it is in, the Earth is smooth and the trees wait for it.
- **Fixed:** the timetable screens in the terminal hall were sunk inside their housings and showed dark; the old English letters showed through behind the bilingual departures sign.
