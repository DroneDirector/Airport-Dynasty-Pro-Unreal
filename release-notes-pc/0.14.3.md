# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.14.3

- Apron light masts: one in front of each of the 8 gates, on the side away from where the jetways turn. 30 m galvanised masts with 8 LED floodlights on a ring, a red obstruction light, bolted base and joints, and a striped W-beam guardrail round the foot.
- They switch on as real aprons do: at sunset, off just after sunrise, and all day in fog and storms, one mast after another. Their beams fan out in the evening haze; the light on the ground is soft.
- Built for speed: one instanced model for all 8 (Nanite on the mast, off for the thin cables and rods), in their own update file.
