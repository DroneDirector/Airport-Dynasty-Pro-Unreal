## Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.10.0: the whole game, one interface

The first PC release built in Unreal Engine 5.7.

- **The game starts on its own title screen**, now marked "PC edition · Unreal Engine 5.7" with the PC version's number.
  Continue, New, Load and Save game and every menu and screen of the airline game (the Overview, fleet, crew, money,
  timetables, settings, cloud sync with your phone) are the same as on Android.
- **The 3D terminal is the Babylon model itself**, imported exactly as it is (nothing rebuilt), with its colours and
  textures and real window glass, and rendered by Unreal (Nanite, Lumen).
- **One way round the whole game**: Terminal opens the 3D terminal, and Airport site puts you outside at the kerb by the
  main entrance. G (or Y / Triangle) goes back to the menus, to the title screen or to the game where you left it.
- **Gamepads in the menus too**: Xbox, PlayStation 5 and other controllers work the menus as on the phone (the pointer,
  the d-pad to jump between buttons, A / Cross to click, B / Circle to go back, the shoulder buttons for the pages).
- **Your airline in the 3D airport**: its name, money and the time under the walking bar. The airport announces your
  game's real departures (boarding, final call, gate closing, delays, cancellations).
- **The walking interface**: the holographic map (M or RB / R1) with routes and a legend; the Buttons panel in the lower
  right (F1 or View / Create); Settings (Tab or Menu / Options) with quality, render scale, frame limit, VSync and every
  control with its symbols for the device in use; Hide UI; the performance overlay (P).
- Still the Android edition's scenes inside the menus: the 3D viewer and the lounge.
