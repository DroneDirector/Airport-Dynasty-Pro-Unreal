# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.17.16

- **The terminal's new parts.** The museum, the escalator room and new offices on Floor 2:
  - **The museum** moved to the west end, in its own hall that reaches the gate walkway. It has tall windows and a double sliding glass door on the kerb side, under a canopy on two pillars like the main entrances'. Everything that was inside the old round museum is in it.
  - **Two escalators side by side,** one up and one down, from the Grand Hall to a new landing on Floor 2. The Grand Hall opens onto them with no wall and no door. Their steps run round endlessly, slide under the comb plates at each end, and carry you up or down; the handrails have smooth rounded curves. The landing has a glass guardrail with black trim and a wide, open way into the gate walkway. The Floor 2 toilets and family room are on the landing now.
  - **The bar** moved along Floor 2 at its full size and is open straight onto the gate walkway, flush with the new offices beside it.
  - **New offices on Floor 2** over the staff wing, reaching the gate walkway, with one big window onto the Grand Hall (ten equal panes, 35 cm of wall at each end), windows to the kerb and the east, and a working staff lift from the staff entrance hall whose doors open by themselves.
  - Chairs only where they belong: in the gate rooms, the view bays and the gate walkway.
- **Your pet.** The character creator's pet step has the real German Shepherd and house cat: the one you pick walks in and sits beside you, and walks back out when you pick another. The dog pants with its tongue out now and then; both breathe and look round. The pet you start with sits outside the terminal's main entrance and now and then walks up to 10 m and back, never into a wall. Every dog or cat added later shows up in its section by itself.
- **A much faster start.** The title screen comes up first, as soon as the game is running; the airport is never shown before it. The roads are no longer built at every start (they come ready-made: 112 roads in a few seconds instead of 30-60), and the title film runs smoothly.
- **A new loading screen** after New game, Continue game or Load game: its bar fills only when everything is really loaded, then "Loading complete · Get ready for takeoff" for four seconds.
- **The update screen** in the new-career neon look, and its progress bar no longer flashes.
- Fixed: the menus could stay black on the first start (their browser timed out while the airport loaded).
