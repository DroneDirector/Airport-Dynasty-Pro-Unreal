# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.17.0

- Drive the crash tenders. Walk up to the driver's door and press E (pad X): you open the door by its handle, climb the step and into the cab holding the handles either side of the doorway, sit down in the middle seat with your hands on the wheel, and the door shuts behind you. E / X again and you climb back out the same way (the truck stops first if it is moving).
- Truck controls: W / S or the pad's triggers to drive and brake (hold the brake at a standstill to reverse), A / D or the left stick to steer, Space / pad A for the parking brake (it lets itself off as you pull away), V / D-pad up between the driver's seat and the view from behind, B / D-pad left for the siren.
- Fire-truck physics: six-wheel drive, springs and dampers on every wheel, front and rear-axle steering, the wheels, arms, springs and drive shafts moving as you drive, the steering wheel and pedals moving with you.
- The cabs: real windows in the doors, open doorways, and on each side a step hung outside the door on two arms (yellow edge marks) and a second step inside, as on the real trucks; a handle either side of each doorway.
- The fire station's bay doors are now sectional doors: shut when you arrive, the one in front of your truck opens as soon as you sit at the wheel (one door per truck leaving), they never come down on a truck or a person in the opening, with their motor's start and stop and its hum as they run. The doors' window bands are see-through glass.
- Sounds: the cab door opening and shutting, the siren, the bay doors.
- Third-person view for your own character: V / D-pad up switches between your eyes and the view from behind (remembered). Both new keys are in the controls list.
- Fixed: the trucks' wheels sat in the bay floor; vehicles and cameras went through the fire station's walls and floor.
