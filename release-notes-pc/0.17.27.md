# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.17.27

- **Title screen without the flicker.** The film behind the title screen is now a real video built into the game (Bink, one made for normal screens and one for 32:9), playing in step with the music. It no longer jumps to catch up with the music several times a second, which was the flashing.
- **Shader cache for smooth nights.** The game now ships a recorded cache of the graphics card's work for the airport by day and by night (4,275 recorded pipelines). The loading screen builds it at the end ("Loading shader cache …%") before you see the airport, so the first lamps at night, the rain or a fire truck's lights no longer stall the game the first time they appear. Anything left after 90 seconds finishes quietly in the background.
- **The Pixel Norma logo** plays again at every start, in full, before the title screen (it is a Bink video now as well).
- **Picking up your phone never shows the title screen any more.** The airline's pages open quietly behind the phone; the title screen only appears when you quit to it.
- **Esc (and the pad's Circle) always put your phone or tablet down**, from up close too. Holding the phone blurs the room behind it like a portrait photo, as the tablet does.
- **The "+" pointer** on screens (the jetway console) now follows how fast you move the mouse: slow and precise, or a quick flick across.
- **Jetway:**
  - The **service door** works: you can walk out onto the stair landing and back in (an invisible block and a lip across the doorway are gone, a step plate added). It opens only with E / X, has an outline when you look at it, and a real steel door's sounds when it opens and shuts.
  - **The cab:** the console's top sits flush with its desk, and the desk is in the console's grey. The window behind the console, the front window facing the aircraft and the windows in both doors are real glass now. The carpet reaches the walls. The walls are in the tunnel's metal plate, the round walls a little darker and more matte.
  - The tunnel's CCTV camera no longer sits on a ceiling lamp.
- **Fire truck:** a real recorded two-tone siren (a seamless loop) and an air horn: H, or L3 on the pad, while you drive (CC0, Freesound).
- **Escalators:** the steps are only 1 % reflective, their black moving parts at most 3 %, and the handrails not at all. The metal keeps its shine.
- **Tax free:** the ceiling reflects far less (about 15 %).
- **The holographic map** is now traced from the real terminal walls, all floors, and shows the jetways where they stand.
- **Staff app** on the computers: who is on the early, late and night shifts, who is off, and hiring for each job, with real-world staffing worked out from your schedule.
- **Updater:** restarting after an update no longer opens your Documents folder.
- **Fixes:** a crash in the tattoo studio is fixed.
