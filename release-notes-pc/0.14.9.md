# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.14.9

- Your smartphone lies on your office desk and in Operations. Take it (E, the controller's X, or click it): it comes up off the desk to you and its apps open in front, in the phone's own frame. Esc, a tap beside it or the button at the bottom (Esc and a phone with an arrow curving down) puts it back down.
- The operations room's monitor, your office's monitor and laptop: use them and the computer opens over the room, its desktop with the taskbar and every app, the operations screens as apps of their own.
- The operations room's monitor matches your own screen: a 49-inch 32:9 super-ultrawide, a 34-inch 21:9 ultrawide or a 27-inch 16:9 widescreen, all high detail, with smooth curved bezels and no streaks on the screen.
- The rooms' furniture is drawn as instances of shared meshes (smoother with many desks and chairs).
- The game keeps its own log, also in the released game: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AirportDynastyPro\Saved\Logs\ADP.log (the run before as ADP-prev.log).
- Not yet: the live picture on the 3D operations monitor itself comes in the next version.
