## Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.9.0: the airfield and its traffic

- **Your airport's airfield round the terminal**, from the same plan as the Android edition: runway 08/26 (4,000 m ×
  60 m) with its centre line, edge lines, threshold bars, designators and touchdown marks; taxiways A and B with their
  links and curves and yellow centre lines; the aprons, the holding positions, the cargo building, and the grass
  inside the airport's boundary.
- **Aircraft at stands 1–8**, each turned round by its ground vehicles (a fuel truck, a catering truck, a baggage tug
  with its carts), then pushed back and taxied out to the holding position, lined up on 08 and off down the runway,
  climbing away. Later they come back on a 3° approach, land, roll out, leave at A2 and taxi in to their stands.
- One aircraft uses the runway at a time, and departures and arrivals keep to different taxiway links.
- **Volumetric clouds** over the airport.
- Everything from 0.5.0 to 0.8.0: the terminal, its passengers, the interface and the airline game with its shared world.
