# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.17.11

**The airfield ground, rebuilt to look real.**

- **Real pavement.** The runway, taxiways and aprons use real photographed asphalt and concrete. The surface no longer repeats in a visible pattern, at any distance, and nothing pops in or out. It has cracks with tar sealant, tyre rubber where the wheels run, worn and dirty paint, and cracks running through the markings.
- **Taxiways shaped like real ones.** Every junction has smooth turn curves, with wider curves where you enter and leave the runway, and smooth fillets onto the runway and the aprons. They have paved shoulders with double side stripes. Along every paved edge the grass, moss and gravel meet the pavement softly, the way they do in real photos.
- **Grass.** The airfield and the terminal's lawn use a real grass scan. The terminal model's lawn is gone, so the grass runs flat under the terminal with no step.
- **Real markings, laid out from the ICAO and FAA diagrams.** Runway-holding positions on a black backing with red runway signs on both sides. Enhanced taxiway centre lines before every hold. Lead-in lines with their turns. Stop bars for each aircraft type. Stand numbers. Red apron boundaries with white edges, restraint lines and the service road. Hatched jetway zones. Every stand gets its own wear.
- **The stands are 12 m further out, and your own jetways reach them.** Your jetways are rebuilt by your own generator. The corridor slopes down 3.3° as before, and the tunnel sections are longer: 10.5, 10.1 and 9.7 m, overlapping 1.5 m. Each jetway docks at the aircraft's front door. The yellow air hoses hang from their own pipes. When the jetways are animated later, they rest half way back.
- **Service roads.** Gate 1 to the control tower, with 8 parking spaces at the tower. The fire station to the apron by gate 1. The fire station onto taxiway A, with a road-holding position and STOP. The roads have white edge lines, a dashed middle and soft grass edges. The fire station has 8 parking spaces on its own paving.
- **Light snow.** There is a Snow setting. Snow lies thin and compacted on the ploughed paving and stays in joints and cracks. There is more on the shoulders and grass, and low banks build up along the edges. It wears off where the wheels run.
- **Sunlight.** Each surface catches the sun in its own way. Asphalt has a low sheen at sunset, concrete is brighter, and the painted markings are the glossiest. The puddles are unchanged.
- The buildings keep their own materials; only the ground uses the new ground materials.

- Install with the Setup file; existing installs update themselves (or use the Update zip). This time there is no separate full zip: it grew past GitHub's 2 GB file limit.
