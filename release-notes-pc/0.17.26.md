# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.17.26

- **The ceiling lamps are back inside.** Since 0.17.17 every room lamp's glowing panel had been drawn half a turn around the terminal - outside, floating in the sky in front of it - while the rooms had none. All of them now hang in their rooms again: the gates, offices, baggage claim, arrivals, tax free and the rest. The game also checks each lamp when the terminal is built, and any lamp with no ceiling over it is taken out.
- **Your tablet and phone in your hands:**
  - Holding the phone or the tablet, all of the game's own UI steps aside; only the Buttons legend stays, and the mouse pointer is free to click it.
  - The tablet's screen shows its whole home screen - app icons, the clock and calendar card, the taskbar - on top of your wallpaper, or its lock screen if you set a code.
  - With a lock code, the code types itself in when you pick the device up, like a saved bank login. There is no screen timeout: the device is always ready.
- **Weather app** on the phone, the tablet and the computers: the real-world forecast for the airport - now, the next 24 hours hour by hour, and the week - with wind strength and direction, and for your work the head- and crosswind on each runway, gusts, visibility and de-icing.
- **Phone tones and voicemail:** choose from 20 ringtones, 20 calling tunes and 20 message tones in the phone's Settings, each with a Play button. You hear your calling tune while it rings at theirs. If nobody answers, their voicemail picks up ("leave a message after the tone"), you hear the beep, and what you say is left as a voice message.
- **Compass** in its own strip right above the small map: N, E, S, W, the points between and tick marks, sliding as you turn, with your true heading in degrees.
- **Fire station doors:** real recordings of a motorised sectional garage door going up and coming down (CC0, Freesound), and the doors now move at the recording's own pace.
- **Jetway CCTV and wheels:** the jetway's drive wheels roll as it drives, at its exact speed. The jetway hoses no longer make the game redraw their whole jetway every frame.
- **Sprint:** tap the run key twice and hold it to sprint at almost twice the running speed - it builds up over a moment, the strides quicken and the view widens a little. A full sprint lasts 15 seconds, then you are back to your normal run; it comes back over about 45 seconds. A **sprint meter** sits in its own strip right under the small map: full and blue when you are rested, going down as you sprint, turning through amber to red as you tire, and filling again. While you sprint flat out you hear yourself breathing hard (a woman's or a man's breath, as your character), and for about 3 seconds after it you catch your breath.
- **Field of view:** one slider now sets walking and driving, first person and from behind; switch on "Separate field of view for each view" to set each on its own.
- **Following a flight in 3D:** Esc (or G) takes you back to the Overview, which you leave to your tablet as usual.
- **Performance log** now counts the pictures shown on screen, so tests report what the game draws and what your screen shows with frame generation.
