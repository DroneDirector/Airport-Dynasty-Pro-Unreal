# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.17.3

- Fire truck lamps look like real lamps: each lens shines with its own glow (headlamps, tail and brake lights, reversing lamps, side markers, the blue lights) without lighting up the truck's own paint around it.
- The headlamps' beams light the road ahead instead of the bumper; the reversing lamps barely touch the ground, as real ones.
- The blue warning lights: a sharp double flash, side to side, their blue thrown outward onto the ground and walls round the truck instead of flooding the cab.
