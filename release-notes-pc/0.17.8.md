# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.17.8

- **Fixed: 0.17.7 crashed while starting** (and could first show a message that the plugin "CameraCalibrationCore" failed to load). Your own character, new as the walking body in 0.17.7, needs two parts of the engine that the game didn't include yet. They're in now, and that plugin is editor-only again, so the message is gone.
- **Your airline is founded on the neon stage.** A new step, **Your airline**, comes before Difficulty: Airline, Regional or Bush outfit, the airline's name, its ICAO code and callsign (filled in from the name until you type your own) and your name. The home base is Mýrdalssandur (BIAP). Everything is already filled in, so with a pad you can just go on with A. After Start game the loading screen stays up until you're in the Overview with your airline; the game's old form is gone.
- **Your pet and your partner on the stage.** Married: your partner walks in from the side and stands beside you; Single: they walk out again. Dog or Cat: until the real 3D pets are made, a neon cat or dog with a question mark above it walks in and sits beside you, in the coat colour you pick (eight per animal, left/right or the pad). The pet you choose goes into the game as your office pet.
- **People:** moustaches. Some men now have a moustache, alone or with a beard, in the colour and grey of their hair. They appear as the people are rebuilt.
