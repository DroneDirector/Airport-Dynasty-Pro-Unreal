# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.18.1

- **Passengers travel through your terminal.** For every flight at your airport they arrive at the big car park, cross at the zebra crossings, check in at their airline's desk and put their own suitcase on the weigh belt. Then they go through security, up the escalators and to their gate, where they sit, lean on a wall or look at their phone or tablet.
  - At the boarding call they walk through the gate door into the jetway and fade away about halfway along (no cabin yet). Arriving passengers come out of the jetway, take their own bag off the claim belt and walk back to the car park.
  - Everyone walks at their own pace. They walk faster when their flight has been called and run after the final call, but always wait their turn in queues and at security.
  - They step aside to let people pass, turning their shoulders in tight gaps, and their legs really step sideways. They turn gently, stay inside the walls, and hold their suitcase by the handle with the fingers closed round it.
  - After about 30 seconds of waiting they move naturally: stretching, checking a watch, looking around, shifting their weight.
- **Bags go all the way.** From the check-in desk, bags drop under the floor into the new departure baggage block (screening, sorting, make-up carousels). The ground crew lift them onto the carts and the tractor tows the train out to the aircraft. The belt loader takes them up into the hold. Arriving bags come the other way, to the claim belts.
- **New ground vehicles:** our own electric baggage tractor, baggage carts that couple into trains by hand, and a belt loader. All ground vehicles get dirty from driving and can be washed.
- **The aircraft waits for its passengers.** The jetway stays at the door until everyone is aboard; a no-show holds the flight for up to 15 minutes. Then the jetway pulls back and the cockpit asks ATC for pushback. Only after the approval does the tug come out to the nose.
- **The terminal:**
  - New check-in layout with 20 desks and two walk-throughs to security.
  - The glass security wall and a new airside corridor in marble to the escalators.
  - The lobby stair and the family room are gone, so Floor 2 is reached by the escalators.
  - Dark wood on the Floor 2 landing and at the top of the escalators.
  - The offices, Operations and the Academy have moved to Floor 2.
  - The entrance doors stay open while passengers are about, and the security doors until the last one is through. The revolving doors can be ridden, one passenger per compartment.
- **The ground power cable** now lies just above the ground and never sinks through it.
- **Settings:** the active graphics quality and frame-rate limit are lit, so you can see what is on. New installs start at High with an 87% render scale; your own saved settings are never changed by an update.
- **New game:** a "Test (no limits)" difficulty ($5 billion and plenty of people to hire). The aircraft market offers only the high-quality A320 for now.
