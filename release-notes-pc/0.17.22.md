# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.17.22

- **Jetways built like the real thing.** The three tunnel sections now telescope in the real-world order: the smallest at the terminal end, the biggest at the cab, where the wheels and lift columns are. Each section is a little larger than the one inside it, above and below, so they slide in and out without ever cutting into each other. Corner tubes along every section, a clean frame where each section meets the next, a floor plate across every joint so the walk stays flat, and the seals and the drive unit follow the new sizes.
- **New jetway finishes.** A light grey metal-plate finish on every wall inside the jetway (rotunda, tunnel and cab), painted metal on the tunnels' outer walls, dark coated fabric on the seals. The rotunda floor is clear (no sign or stripe across it).
- **Neon loading screen everywhere.** The loading screen into the airport is now the same neon screen as when you start a new game: the glowing title, the neon bar, what is loading and the tips.
- **Controls in groups.** Settings > Controls lists every key and button by group: on foot, driving, fire truck, flying, at the airport, menus.
- **World terrain download.** Faster (four packs at once), it carries on where it stopped instead of failing, and the counter counts up as the data comes in.
- **Less flicker with our frame generation.** No in-between pictures while a menu, Settings, the map or a device screen is open, and glass and screens no longer smear.
- **The Pixel Norma logo** plays when the game starts.
