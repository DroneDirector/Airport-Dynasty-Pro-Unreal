## Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.8.0: the airline game and the shared world on the PC

- **The whole airline game in the PC version** (G, or the GAME button): the Overview, the fleet, crew, money,
  timetables, every menu and screen, the very same game code as on Android, so the rules are always the same.
- **One world on the phone and the PC**: through the game's cloud sync the PC joins your airline (Settings → sync on
  the phone, then the short code on the PC). One device runs the world at a time; the other shows it live and takes
  over as soon as you use it. Your airline is encrypted on your devices before it goes online.
- G or Esc takes you back to the terminal, where everything from 0.5.0 to 0.7.0 is: the terminal to walk around in,
  its passengers, the holographic map and the settings.
