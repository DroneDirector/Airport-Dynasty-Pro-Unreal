# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.17.23

- **New escalators.** Every escalator is new, built after real airport escalators: the black handrail runs as a real loop - along the top, round a smooth round newel at both ends (on the ground floor and on the upper floor) and back underneath into a black rubber inlet. The glass sits inside the handrail all the way round. Matte grooved steps with yellow edges, brushed stainless at real size (no stretching, no seams), screws on the floor plates, emergency stops, key switches, skirt brushes, direction lights and anti-slide studs. The steps keep running as before.
- **Crown lights.** A big stainless crown light hangs on steel cables over the Grand Hall escalators and over the arrivals escalators, glowing warm.
- **Arrivals escalators open onto the gate walkway.** At the top of the baggage claim escalators you now walk straight out onto the gate walkway; the tax free is smaller (it ends at gate 11) and has its entrance at its new end; the landing floor is the walkway's oak.
- **Big advert screen** on the wall beside the top of the arrivals escalators.
- **Glass without blocks.** The terminal's glass no longer shows square patches.
- **Hangar doors** telescope the real way: the outer leaf travels furthest.
- **Baggage belts:** the hood's walls and its rubber strips reach down to the belt; the plates tip slightly in the bends so you see every plate; the metal no longer stretches.
- **Escalator tops:** the wood landing floor no longer runs over the tops of the arrivals escalators; it stops at their sides.
- **The stair beside the arrivals escalators** now has the escalators' ribbed metal steps with yellow edges instead of wood, and its glass guard is one smooth rail with a round corner at the top.
- **Escalator steps** have dark grooves between their ribs, like a real escalator.
- **Escalator glass:** every escalator in the airport has the same smoked glass, so no pane looks clear next to the tinted ones; the arrivals stair's guard matches it.
