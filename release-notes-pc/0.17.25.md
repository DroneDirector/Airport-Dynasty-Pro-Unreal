# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.17.25

- **Jetway CCTV.** Every jetway now carries five real CCTV cameras, modelled after real airport cameras and moving with the part they're mounted on:
  1. **Bogie** - a dome under the last tunnel section, watching the drive wheels.
  2. **Aircraft door** - a dome on the cab's ceiling, looking at the aircraft door, with yellow docking guide lines over the picture.
  3. **Apron** - a dome under the cab's front, looking ahead and down.
  4. **Bridge exterior** - a white bullet camera with a sun shield on the first tunnel's roof, looking along the bridge to the cab.
  5. **Tunnel** - a dome on the tunnel's ceiling, looking down the walkway.
- **Live pictures on the jetway screen.** The HMI's CCTV page shows all five cameras live, looking like real CCTV: wide-angle, slightly soft and grainy, muted colour, with the camera name and date/time burnt in. Click any picture to show it full screen; click again to go back to all five. The pictures are only taken while the CCTV page is open, so they cost nothing the rest of the time.
- **Lock code and auto-lock** on your tablet, phone and computers (their Settings): set a 4-digit code (entered twice), and choose auto-lock after 1, 5 or 15 minutes untouched - or never. With a code, the device asks for it when you pick it up and when it has locked itself; without one, a tap opens the lock screen. The lock screen shows the time and date over your wallpaper.
