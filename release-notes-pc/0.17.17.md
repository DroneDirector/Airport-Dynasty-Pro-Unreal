# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.17.17

- **Fixed: the crash on "Who are you?"** when you came back to it (a material the stage kept was thrown away by the
  game's memory clean-up; every such object is now kept).
- **The escalators are in the game** (they were left out of the package), and you no longer get stuck stepping on at
  the bottom, stepping off at the top, or getting onto the down escalator from Floor 2: their ends sit flush with the
  floor.
- **Also back in the game:** the road signs, street lights, the land along the roads, the fire trucks' paint and glass,
  the glow round things you can use, and the apron's wear.
- **The stairs to Floor 2** in the airside hall are open (a floor and a ceiling had closed them off), with herringbone
  wood upstairs and a glass guardrail with black trim round the stairwell. Floors on Floor 2 you could fall through
  are solid.
- **Your pet** sits on the pavement outside the main entrance (it was sunk into the ground).
- **No more stutter every 15 seconds.** The seats were looked for again in every building every 15 seconds, which
  froze the game for about a tenth of a second; now only when a building is added. The game also runs a little faster on
  foot: the sun's shadows reach 2.6 km while you walk or drive (42 km when flying or in the drone) and lamps are drawn up
  to 150 m (600 m flying), switched by themselves. The roads are drawn the faster Nanite way.
- **Settings in the neon look**: categories down the left (Graphics, Sound, World, Controls, Updates, Perf log, About),
  the chosen one's options beside them.
- **After New game, Continue or Load** you go straight into the airport on foot once everything is loaded (the loading
  bar no longer starts at its end), and the title screen's buttons light up the moment the mouse is over them.
- **Super-ultrawide screens**: the character creator keeps the whole figure and your pet in view.
- **Stress walk** on the title screen: the game walks the airport by itself and measures how smoothly it runs, then
  shows what it found (with Copy). Every very slow frame is written to Saved/Logs/ADP-Perf.log with its cause.
