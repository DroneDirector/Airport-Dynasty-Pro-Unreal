# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.13.6

## New seating at the gates
- Every row of seats in the terminal (the gates, the airside hall, the view bays, the walkways and the landing: 52 rows, 1,506 places) is now detailed beam seating: shell seats and reclined backs in the gates' dark blue, on X-shaped legs whose upper arms are the armrests, black hubs where the legs cross and round floor pads; legs, arms and beam in 75% matte chrome steel. All of it Nanite.

## Less washed out indoors
- The gates and the rest of the terminal look less washed out: a little less light leaking in and bouncing about, more shading where things meet, and the camera a touch less bright indoors.
