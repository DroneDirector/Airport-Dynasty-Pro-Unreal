## Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.6.0: people in the terminal

- **400 passengers** walk through the terminal, each on a departing passenger's trip: in from the kerb, to a check-in
  desk, through a security lane, a stop at the Food Walk, Tax Free, the bar or a seat, on to their gate to wait,
  then aboard through the jetway or the walk-out door. When one has boarded, a new passenger arrives at the kerb.
- They find their own way through the terminal's doors and up and down the stairs, the shortest way, from the same
  layout as the Android edition.
- People far from you are redrawn less often, so a busy terminal stays smooth.
- Everything from 0.5.0: the whole terminal to walk around in, its doors, rooms, stairs, lifts, jetways and screens.
