# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.17.19

- **Jetways move.** They drive out to the aircraft and back at real jetway speeds, with the tunnel, cab, stair, bellows and floor plates moving together. At rest they stand half way back.
- **Jetway controls.** Press E at the control panel in the cab for Dock and Undock, or use the jetway button in the lower right corner.
- **Jetway lights.** Red turning beacons on the cab roof, amber beacons under the cab and at the wheels, floodlights at night, a light over the doorway, a docking lamp for the cabin crew, and a softly glowing gate sign.
- **Jetway sounds.** A warning beeper, the drive motor, the tyres and the lift. You hear them up to 250 m away, muffled inside the jetway and in the terminal.
- **Cab doors** open when docked and shut before the jetway drives away.
- **Four escalators** in the escalator room.
