# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.18.2

- **The car park's entry and exit redesigned:** a give-way junction onto the road to town, two entry and two exit barrier lanes, new inside lanes, footways to the crossings and pay machines - while losing far fewer spaces than first planned (164 lost instead of 224).
- **Curved gate windows** on every Floor 2 gate (gates 1-4, 9, 11, 13-16) and every future gate.
- Gate 13's swing lobby door now counts as a passenger door, so passengers on their way to gates 13-16 use it properly.
- Fixed a bug that could leave the car park's walking routes unreliable right after loading - passengers now always find their way across it correctly.
