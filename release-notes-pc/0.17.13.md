# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.17.13

- **The terminal is now a true Unreal build.** Your terminal, the car rental and everything in them are now made from Unreal's own parts. Your shapes, pictures and colours are exactly as before.
  - **Built from Unreal's own pieces:** 1,465 pieces. Every copy of a piece (window bays, columns, seats, lamps) is drawn as one instanced Nanite mesh. That's faster, and the terminal's files went from 426 MB to 119 MB.
  - **Real Unreal materials:** surfaces get fine real detail (fabric weave, carpet pile, wood grain, leather, plaster and concrete) and the right shine. Polished floors reflect the lamps and brushed steel has its streaks.
  - **Real glass:** clear when you look straight through, turning into a mirror toward a glancing angle like real windows, with reflections of the sky, the apron and the lights.
  - **Lamps, signs and screens** keep their pictures and brightness.
  - **Nothing comes from the Babylon export any more.** The old copy isn't packed into the game, which makes the download smaller.
- **The yellow "GATE n" signs** that hung at each gate's window are gone.
- **Fire trucks:**
  - **No more flickering** surfaces.
  - **Real lights:** headlights (low and high beam), tail, brake and reverse lights, indicators, side markers, and a roof light bar flashing in a real rhythm, its colours easy to see by day and night.
  - **Real paint:** a clear-coat finish with wear and chips, wet in the rain.
  - **Dirt:** only from driving off the paving, or in wet weather. They start clean.
  - **Top speed 135 km/h**, with the power of a big airport crash tender.
  - **Working gauges:** the speedometer, rev counter, temperature, oil, fuel and volts needles move as you drive, and the dials glow softly at night.
- **The fire station's bay doors** open and close only with a button: **O** on the keyboard, **D-pad right** on a pad, within 25 m of the station. It shows in the Buttons list only when you're close enough. The doors sound as they start, run and stop.
- **One ground everywhere:**
  - The fire station's own ground sheets are gone. It stands level on the airfield's paving, flush with its floor, so trucks roll out without a bump.
  - The extra ground disc round the control tower is gone too.
- **The wash hose works:**
  - **Taking it:** walk up to the hose on the fire station wall and press **E** / **X**. You see your arm reach out and take the spray gun.
  - **Carrying it:** the hose pays out of the wall coil loop by loop as you walk away (about 24 m of it), fully flexible on the ground, and winds back on as you come back.
  - **Spraying:** hold the **left mouse button** / **right trigger** for a continuous jet of water, whitening as it breaks up, with spray clouds, splashes and wet patches on the ground.
  - **Washing:** where it hits a truck, the dirt comes off gradually and runs down. Beads of water stay on the paint and windows, dry in about 20 minutes, and blow off when you drive.
  - **Hanging it up:** walk back to the mount and press **E** / **X**. Your arm hangs the gun back on its hook.
  - **Hangars:** every hangar has the same hose on its inside wall by the big doors, and so does every hangar or fire station you place later.
- **Outlines:** everything you can use or pick up shows a thin soft glow round its edge when you're within about 4 m. That covers your phone, the computers, the ops monitors, the wardrobe, the hangar door buttons, the hose and the seats. Doors and vehicles don't glow.
- **Sit anywhere:** press **E** / **X** by any chair, stool, armchair, bench or gate seat in the terminal, the offices, the ops room or the lounge, and you sit down. Look around freely, and press **E** / **X** again to stand up.
- Install with the Setup file. Existing installs update themselves, or you can use an Update zip.
