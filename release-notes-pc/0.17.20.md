# Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.17.20

- **Talk to people.** Look at someone and press E to talk to them. Each person answers in their own way, and some keep personal things to themselves. You can also ask them to work for you, and a few will say yes.
- **Unruly passengers.** Try to calm them down, or call airport security. Security can call the police, and the police handle it from there.
- **Ride the jetway.** You move with the jetway as it drives, even while walking inside it.
- **Outlines.** People get the same outline as things you can use, and an outline goes away while you're using the thing.
