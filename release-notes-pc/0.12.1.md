## Airport Dynasty Pro (Unreal) 0.12.1: walking, the mouse and Escape

- **Walking works**: W A S D (and the arrows) walk again in the airport; nothing in the interface takes the keys away
  any more, and leaving the menus gives the keyboard straight back to the game.
- **The middle mouse button** frees the pointer (to click the bars and drag boxes) and, pressed again, always gives you
  the view back.
- **Escape** works as in a released game: in the 3D world it closes the open panel (the map, Settings); with nothing
  open it takes you to the Overview. In the Overview it asks before leaving to the title screen: Save game, Yes or No
  (Save game opens the save slots).
- **The real sun**: where it really is over the airport for the date and the time of day, low and golden in the
  winter afternoon, gone at night; the sky's light fills the shadows.
- **A test bar at the top** (for testing): the time of day (the sun moves) and eight weather themes (clear, few
  clouds, broken, overcast, fog, rain, storm, snow; rain and snow only darken the sky for now).
- **Updates from inside the game**: Settings → Updates → Check for updates. From now on, new versions install from
  there, downloading only what changed (this version is the base the updates build on).
- **The 2D map**: your airport, Mýrdalssandur (BIAP), is always shown, at every zoom, with its code over it.
- The trees round the car park are gone for now; the rows at the kerbs stay until real trees replace them.
- The credits sit in one line at the bottom, never on the map or the Buttons panel.
- Fixed: the release build could crash when the globe loaded terrain (the globe's own credit display wasn't packaged).
